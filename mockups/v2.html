How it works

Clipper Conquest is played by two teams that race to conquer as many SF neighborhoods as possible. Whichever team has more neighborhoods when the clock hits zero is the winner.

Every one of San Francisco's 41 neighborhoods has 3 unique challenges. You can conquer a neighborhood by completing its challenges. But be careful, because the other team can steal the neighborhood by completing more of the neighborhood's challenges than you.

If you only complete one challenge, you conquer the neighborhood quickly but are vulnerable to a steal. If you complete all three, the neighborhood is locked, but it might take a long time to complete all three challenges.

  1. Two teams, Red vs Blue, 2–6 players each.
  2. Stay together. Split up only when a challenge explicitly says to.
  3. Any team can attempt any challenge at any time.
  4. Ties go to whoever got there first. On an equal count, the team whose completion was logged in the app first holds the neighborhood.
  5. Photo proof with every completion. Honor system. Log the completion as soon as you can; the photo can follow when it uploads.
  6. One-shots: a miss is a miss. Everything else takes persistence.
  7. Some challenges may be impossible on the day (something closed). Move on.
  8. Muni only. No BART, no Caltrain, no cars or bikes. No running unless a challenge says to — speedwalk.
  9. No law-breaking. Pay fares, cross on the signal, no trespassing.
  10. No interfering with the other team — no blocking, distracting or sabotaging their challenges.
  11. Questions about a challenge? Text the group chat.
  12. When the clock hits zero, a challenge still in progress counts as failed. Tied on neighborhoods? The team that first held that many wins.
  13. Be kind and stay safe.

Encouraged, not required: take lots of photos and videos, and share the spotlight — a different teammate for each challenge.
