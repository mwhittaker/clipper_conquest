Clipper Conquest
five design pitches

Each one renders the same game moment — Team Fog 4, Team Surf 3, the Mission one flip away — so you're comparing design, not content. Open each on your phone; that's the screen it's designed for.

guidePre-game guide

Desktop study guide: rules at a glance, the 41-neighborhood map with a Muni rail + rapid overlay, all 123 challenges to read through, and a printable one-page challenge sheet.
v2Layout prototype

The current direction: real SF neighborhood boundaries, score + clock on top, map filling the screen, tap for a full-screen challenge list, swipe between neighborhoods. Unstyled on purpose.
1Race Control

Live-broadcast sports graphics: dark control room, chunky chyron scoreboard, breaking-news ticker. The Jet Lag episode look.
2Expedition Ledger

Vintage campaign cartography: aged paper, engraved type, hatched territories, a rubber "HELD BY SURF" stamp.
3Wayfinding

Transit-system signage: black station-sign bars, route badges, an amber next-train countdown. The game as public infrastructure.
4Skirmish

Retro handheld strategy game: pixel dither territories, HP-bar scores, a quest-log sheet, faint scanlines.
5Karl

Soft SF-fog modern: frosted glass, gentle depth, coral vs teal. The one that could ship in the App Store tomorrow.
Both team-color pairs in every pitch pass colorblind-separation and contrast checks. The map is a stylized SF, not exact geography.
