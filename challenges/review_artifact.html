1Bayview Hunters Point

Challenges anchor on those three zones: the corridor, the hill, and the shoreline.

Shadow O'Clock

Guess, then measure

10–15 min · $0

Climb to Hilltop Park and its giant yellow sundial (the shadow-casting arm is 70 feet long). Phones and watches away. Read the time from the sundial, convert it to clock time yourselves (hint: think about daylight saving), and write your answer down. Then check a phone: within 15 minutes = complete. Off by more = wait 10 minutes and read it again. No shadow because of fog? Everyone touches the gnomon at once instead.

📍 Hilltop Park, Whitney Young Circle at Newcomb Ave.

📷 A teammate standing on the shadow line holding up the written time.

Mural Memory on Third Street

Memory

10 min · $0

Find the Tuzuri Watu mural ("We Are Beautiful People," 1987) at Third & Palou. Everyone studies it for 60 seconds, then the whole team turns its back. Three fixed questions, answered as a team: (1) how many human faces are painted, (2) how many of them wear something on their head, (3) what color is the largest figure's clothing. Turn around and check by counting together. Three for three = complete. Any miss = a new 60 seconds and try again.

📍 Third St & Palou Ave (the Obama mural at Third & Oakdale, two blocks on, works as a second board with the same three questions).

📷 Backs to the wall mid-answer, then the recount with fingers on faces.

Count the Quesada Palms

Mind meld

5–10 min · $0

Quesada Avenue between Third St and Newhall St has a strip of towering palm trees down its center median, wrapped by the famous Quesada community gardens. Two teammates become the counters: one walks the north sidewalk, one the south, each silently counting every palm trunk in the median. At the Newhall end they say their numbers at the same time. A match = complete. A mismatch = swap sidewalks and count again. (Team of two: you're both counters.)

📍 Quesada Ave median, Third St to Newhall St

📷 Both counters holding up their matching number on fingers at the Newhall end, the palm boulevard behind.

Read the giant Hilltop Park sundial and convert it to clock time, take a five-question memory quiz on the Tuzuri Watu mural, and send two counters down opposite sidewalks of Quesada Avenue until their palm counts match. A deduction puzzle, a memory game, an agreement game; nothing failable, zero strangers, all $0, ≈ 35–45 minutes. (Flora Grubb's Price Is Right and the Heron's Head walk stay as alternates.)

Cardboard Curling at the Twin Slides

Aim & throw

10–15 min · $0

Scavenger & find

Word game
