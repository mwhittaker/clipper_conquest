# Mission Bay

The newest-built neighborhood in San Francisco — a literal bay filled in with rubble
(some of it 1906 earthquake debris), now home to Chase Center's art-filled plaza, UCSF's
biotech campus, and Mission Creek's improbable houseboat colony with its herons and
kayakers. Boundary note, verified against the official DataSF Analysis Neighborhoods
polygon: **Oracle Park IS inside Mission Bay** (the northern boundary is Townsend St, so
the ballpark, Willie Mays Plaza, and the 4th & King Caltrain station all belong to this
neighborhood, not Financial District/South Beach; the southern boundary is Mariposa St).
Anchors: Chase Center/Thrive City, China Basin Park (opened 2024, with SF's newest
beach), the Oracle Park perimeter, and Mission Creek.

## Candidates

### 1. Trapped in the Infinity Mirror
- **Do:** At *Seeing Spheres* — the ring of five 15.5-foot mirrored steel spheres on the
  Chase Center east plaza — take ONE photo in which every teammate's face is visible in a
  single sphere's mirror, **including the photographer** (it's a mirror shot, so the
  camera-holder appears too). Complete when one photo shows the whole team reflected.
- **Where:** Thrive City plaza, east entrance of Chase Center (Terry A. Francois Blvd at
  Warriors Way).
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (rearrange and reshoot until everyone fits)
- **Type:** Frame the shot
- **Photo:** The whole team inside one giant mirror, ringed by LED lights, with the other
  spheres reflecting to infinity behind them.
- **Why here:** Olafur Eliasson's largest public artwork in the United States lives on
  this plaza, outdoors and free, 24/7.

### 2. Round the Horn, Then Hit the Beach
- **Do:** At China Basin Park, every teammate runs the bases on the Barry Bonds Junior
  Giants Field — the t-ball-sized mini diamond — and touches home plate. Then the whole
  team walks to the park's sand beach on McCovey Cove and every member touches the sand.
  Complete when all teammates have done both. (If a game is using the field, wait or
  come back — it's a public park.)
- **Where:** China Basin Park, across McCovey Cove from Oracle Park (Terry A. Francois
  Blvd at Toni Stone Crossing).
- **Time:** ~10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Body
- **Photo:** A teammate crossing home plate mid-sprint with Oracle Park rising across the
  water behind them.
- **Why here:** A pint-size ballpark in the shadow of the real one, next to the newest
  beach in San Francisco — both opened in 2024 in SF's newest neighborhood.

### 3. Bronze Age Charades
- **Do:** Oracle Park is ringed by five Giants legends in bronze: Willie Mays (3rd & King
  main entrance), Orlando Cepeda and Gaylord Perry (facing each other at 2nd & King),
  Juan Marichal mid-leg-kick (Lefty O'Doul Plaza, by the 3rd St bridge), and Willie
  McCovey (China Basin Park, across the cove). Walk the statues and study their poses as
  long as you like. Then one teammate — the performer — secretly puts all five names in
  a random order (shuffle them on paper or a phone). Without words, the performer acts
  out each statue's pose in that order, and the rest of the team names each one. Any
  wrong guess = the performer reshuffles and starts over from the first statue. Complete
  when the team names all five in a row.
- **Where:** Oracle Park perimeter, 2nd & King to 3rd & King, plus China Basin Park;
  perform anywhere along the way (Willie Mays Plaza is the classic stage).
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (reshuffle and start over until you get all five)
- **Type:** Act it, guess it
- **Photo:** The performer frozen mid-Marichal-leg-kick in front of a bronze, team
  pointing and shouting the answer.
- **Why here:** No other neighborhood has a ballpark moat of Giants legends — and the
  official boundary puts all of them in Mission Bay.

### 4. Creek Patrol
- **Do:** Walk the public Mission Creek Park path on the south bank of Mission Creek
  (between the 4th St and 6th St bridges) and get ONE photo with at least TWO wild
  birds in the same frame, on or over the creek (heron, egret, cormorant, pelican,
  duck and gull all count — the creek is a bird highway). Complete when you have the
  shot.
- **Where:** Mission Creek Park path, south bank of the channel, Channel St between 4th
  and 6th.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (birds rotate through constantly; keep watching)
- **Type:** Frame the shot
- **Photo:** Two birds sharing one frame over the last surviving sliver of the old bay.
- **Why here:** Mission Creek is the last surviving sliver of the original bay, and it's
  still a bird highway through a brand-new high-rise neighborhood.

### 5. Join the Knothole Gang
- **Do:** Walk the Portwalk — the free public promenade along McCovey Cove behind Oracle
  Park's right-field wall — and find the fenced archway where the modern "Knothole Gang"
  can watch real games for free, three innings at a time. Take a photo THROUGH the
  chain-link with the field (grass or seats) clearly visible, old-timey
  peeking-through-the-fence style. Complete when the through-the-fence shot exists.
- **Where:** McCovey Cove Portwalk, along the water behind right field (enter from
  the 3rd St / Lefty O'Doul Bridge end or from 2nd St).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Frame the shot
- **Photo:** Faces pressed to the fence, a big-league field framed in an archway, bay at
  your backs.
- **Why here:** Oracle Park deliberately revived the 1900s "knothole gang" tradition —
  it's one of the only MLB parks where watching baseball is free.

### 6. Tip Your Cap on Toni Stone Crossing
- **Do:** Find the street sign for **Toni Stone Crossing**, the street along China Basin
  Park named for the first woman ever to play professional big-league baseball (she
  broke in with the Negro Leagues' Indianapolis Clowns in 1953). Before shooting, one
  teammate must read her one-line story (on your phone or a park sign) aloud to the
  team. Photo: the whole team under the sign, every member tipping a cap (real or
  invisible). Complete when the sign is legible in the team cap-tip photo.
- **Where:** Toni Stone Crossing at Terry A. Francois Blvd / Bridgeview Way, Mission
  Rock, south edge of China Basin Park.
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** Team mid-cap-tip beneath the Toni Stone Crossing sign, ballpark in the
  distance.
- **Why here:** Mission Rock's brand-new streets honor trailblazing women — a "wait,
  that exists?" piece of baseball history hiding on a street sign.

## Recommended trio

**1, 3, 4** — The mirror shot at Chase Center, statue charades around the ballpark
(shuffle, perform, name all five in a row), and a two-birds-one-frame hunt on Mission
Creek. One photo puzzle, one teammate guessing game, one patience hunt; nothing
failable, zero strangers, all $0 and outdoors, ≈ 35–45 minutes.
