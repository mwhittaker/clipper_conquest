# Bayview Hunters Point

The sunny southeast corner of the city: the Third Street commercial corridor with its
murals and the 1888 Bayview Opera House, Hunters Point hill rising above it with a
70-foot sundial on top, and a working-waterfront edge that's quietly become great
parkland — Heron's Head Park (a wetland peninsula literally shaped like a heron's head)
and the brand-new India Basin Waterfront Park around an 1875 shipbuilder's cottage.
Challenges anchor on those three zones: the corridor, the hill, and the shoreline.

## Candidates

### 1. Shadow O'Clock
- **Do:** Climb to Hilltop Park and its giant yellow sundial (the shadow-casting arm is
  70 feet long). Phones and watches away. Read the time from the sundial, convert it to
  clock time yourselves (hint: think about daylight saving), and write your answer down.
  Then check a phone: within 15 minutes = complete. Off by more = wait 10 minutes and
  read it again. No shadow because of fog? Everyone touches the gnomon at once instead.
- **Where:** Hilltop Park, Whitney Young Circle at Newcomb Ave.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (read again until you're close)
- **Type:** Guess, then measure
- **Photo:** A teammate standing on the shadow line holding up the written time.
- **Why here:** A 70-foot sundial on a Bayview hilltop, and most people forget it's an
  hour off all summer.

### 2. Walk the Heron's Beak
- **Do:** Heron's Head Park is a peninsula that, from the air, is shaped like a heron's
  head. Walk the trail (~3/4 mile) all the way to the tip of the "beak" — the very end
  of the point, where the trail runs out into the Bay. On the way, spot 3 different
  kinds of birds (over 100 species live here; any 3 visibly different birds count —
  name them however you like). Complete when you reach the end of the trail.
- **Where:** Heron's Head Park, entrance at Cargo Way & Jennings St
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Go there
- **Photo:** Whole team at the trail's end at the beak's tip, Bay and skyline behind
- **Why here:** SF's only bird-shaped park, built on an abandoned shipping terminal
  that turned itself into a wetland.

### 3. Skip a Stone at India Basin
- **Do:** At India Basin Waterfront Park, walk down to the shoreline (the gravel and sand
  edge by the old boat launch). Find flat stones and skip: the challenge is complete when
  any teammate gets a stone to skip THREE times on the bay — three visible touches
  before it sinks. Everyone throws; unlimited tries; keep the throws away from birds and
  people.
- **Where:** India Basin Waterfront Park shoreline, 900 Innes Ave at Griffith St
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (keep skipping)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** The thrower mid-release with the rings on the water, the Shipwright's
  Cottage (1875) behind.
- **Why here:** A $200M park opened in 2024 around SF's last shipbuilder's cottage, with
  the calmest patch of bay on the east side — skipping water.

### 4. Mural Memory on Third Street
- **Do:** Find the Tuzuri Watu mural ("We Are Beautiful People," 1987) at Third & Palou.
  Everyone studies it for 60 seconds, then the whole team turns its back. Three fixed
  questions, answered as a team: (1) how many human faces are painted, (2) how many of
  them wear something on their head, (3) what color is the largest figure's clothing.
  Turn around and check by counting together. Three for three = complete. Any miss = a
  new 60 seconds and try again.
- **Where:** Third St & Palou Ave (the Obama mural at Third & Oakdale, two blocks on,
  works as a second board with the same three questions).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (new study round until three for three)
- **Type:** Memory
- **Photo:** Backs to the wall mid-answer, then the recount with fingers on faces.
- **Why here:** The Third Street corridor is Bayview's living room, and this is its most
  famous wall — dense enough to punish a lazy memory.

### 5. The Price Is Right at Flora Grubb
- **Do:** Flora Grubb Gardens is a jungle-like nursery that's free to wander. One
  teammate, the host, picks any plant and covers its price tag with a hand. Everyone
  else writes down a price guess. Closest guess WITHOUT going over wins — and the
  challenge is complete if that guess is within 20% of the real price. Nobody close, or
  everyone over? New host, new plant, try again.
- **Where:** Flora Grubb Gardens, 1634 Jerrold Ave (open Sat–Sun 10am–6pm).
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (new plant until someone's close)
- **Type:** Guess, then measure
- **Photo:** The host dramatically revealing the tag, guesses held up beside it.
- **Why here:** A nursery with a car planted full of succulents and plants that cost
  more than a scooter — nobody's price instincts survive it.

### 6. Count the Quesada Palms
- **Do:** Quesada Avenue between Third St and Newhall St has a strip of towering palm
  trees down its center median, wrapped by the famous Quesada community gardens. Two
  teammates become the counters: one walks the north sidewalk, one the south, each
  silently counting every palm trunk in the median. At the Newhall end they say their
  numbers at the same time. A match = complete. A mismatch = swap sidewalks and count
  again. (Team of two: you're both counters.)
- **Where:** Quesada Ave median, Third St to Newhall St
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (swap sides and recount until you agree)
- **Type:** Mind meld
- **Photo:** Both counters holding up their matching number on fingers at the Newhall
  end, the palm boulevard behind.
- **Why here:** A palm-boulevard-turned-neighborhood-garden that residents built
  themselves — Bayview's most photogenic residential block, with just enough palms to
  make two people disagree.

## Recommended trio

**1, 4, 6** — Read the giant Hilltop Park sundial and convert it to clock time, take a
five-question memory quiz on the Tuzuri Watu mural, and send two counters down opposite
sidewalks of Quesada Avenue until their palm counts match. A deduction puzzle, a memory
game, an agreement game; nothing failable, zero strangers, all $0, ≈ 35–45 minutes.
(Flora Grubb's Price Is Right and the Heron's Head walk stay as alternates.)
