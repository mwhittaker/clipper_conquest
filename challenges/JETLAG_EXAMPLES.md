## Au$tralia (59)

- **Make A Rock Engraving At The Indigenous Rock Engravings** — Visit the indigenous rock engravings by North Bondi. Then, one team member must make a rock engraving of their own. Generate a random animal from the list provided and carve it into a rock that no one cares about. The other team member must guess the animal correctly on their first try.
- **Score an Australian Rules Football Goal In Redfern**
- **Win a Carnival Game at Luna Park** — You have $20 worth of attempts to win a prize at any of the carnival games that only have one prize tier (buckets, backboard, basketball, or cans.)
- **Taste Test Australian Foods** — Buy 5 iconic Australian foods (Vegemite, Anzac biscuits, Sirena Tuna, Caramello Koala, Tim Tam) and feed [3 of them] in one bite to your blindfolded partner, who must then identify which 3 food they ate.
- **Throw a Democracy Sausage Into A Bun** — From a distance of 30 feet, throw a democracy sausage into a bun. One team member will throw the sausage and one team member must catch it in the bun. They may not touch the sausage with their hands when catching. You get 5 attempts.
- **Successfully Throw and Catch a Boomerang**
- **Pass Amy's Australian Slang Quiz** — Amy has written a quiz on Australian slang terms. Take the quiz and pass it.
- **Go Surfing At Bondi or Manly Beach** — At either Bondi or Manly Beach, one team member must stand up on a surfboard while moving forward on a wave. You have three hours after receiving your board to try. Any stance where you are on your two feet unsupported by any other part of your body counts. You must stand for four seconds.
- **Read Signal Flags At Mount Nelson Signal Station** — At the historic Mount Nelson Signal Station, one team member must create and use signal flags to spell a random 10 letter word, which the other team member must decode from a distance of 100 feet. The signaling may not last more than 100 seconds. The decoder has one guess.
- **Create a Traditional Tasmanian Kelp Basket**
- **Throw a Shrimp on the Barbie** — Acquire shrimp and a Barbie. From a distance of 30 feet, throw shrimp on the Barbie. You have 100 tries to land any part of the shrimp on any part of the Barbie.
- **That's Not A Knife** — Cut a Tim Tam in half using something that's not a knife, without breaking the Tim Tam. When finished, you must have two intact pieces with the full area of a Tim Tam. Crumbs are acceptable.
- **Calculate Boba** — Bubble tea is a popular snack all across Oceania. Order one bubble tea at any bubble tea vendor. From the second you pick up your drink, you have one minute to examine it and determine the number of boba inside. Drink the tea and empty out the boba to check your answer. If you were more than 10% off, this challenge is failed.
- **Get Goosebumps Near a Goose** — Film a shot, at least 10 seconds long, of one teammate having visible goosebumps with at least one goose also in the frame. If the goose leaves the frame, the contract is automatically voided. You must complete this challenge in 45 minutes.
- **Buy The Same Thing As Your Teammate** — Pick any store. One teammate will go in, purchase one item, and then hand it to their blindfolded teammate. The blindfolded teammate may inspect the item as much as they want, then has one attempt to purchase the same item.
- **Locate a Looney Tune At Movie World** — Visit Movie World. After entering the park, you have 1 hour to take a photo with any Looney Tunes mascot. This must be a live mascot with a person inside it. This may not be part of a scheduled show or parade.
- **Mind Meld At The Brisbane Sign** — One team member must, without giving any prior hints or information, select one letter of the Brisbane sign. The other team member gets three attempts to guess which letter their partner selected.
- **Land A Bottle Right Side Up (Upside Down)** — You're upside down right now! Land any disposable bottle right side up (on its cap) after throwing it. You get 10 attempts.
- **Keep Calm on The Drop at Dreamworld**
- **Measure a Mile on the Wheel of Brisbane**
- **Predict Market Flow at Adelaide's Central Market** — Adelaide's Central Market is the one of the largest produce markets in the southern hemisphere. Select any market stall and make a prediction about how many customers it will get in the next ten minutes. A customer is defined as any person who spends money at the stall. If your prediction is more than 40% off, this challenge is failed. If no one visits the stall in the 10 minutes you lose automatically.
- **Toss a Tempting Meal** — Throw a single piece of food. Within 10 seconds of it touching the ground (or water), something besides you must eat it. If the food is not eaten after 10 seconds, this challenge is failed.
- **Face Tim Tam** — Place a Tim Tam on your forehead. You must guide it into your mouth without touching it with anything other than your face. If the Tim Tam falls, this challenge is failed.
- **Distinguish Wines at the National Wine Centre of Australia** — Visit the National Wine Centre of Australia. One team member must purchase 3 glasses of wine (a red, a white, and a rose) and bring them to the other team member, who is blindfolded. The blindfolded team member must successfully guess which one is which. You may not practice. No sparkling wines. Ask them to please put them all in identical glasses.
- **Find 5 Different Fish in 5 Minutes at Port Noarlunga Reef** — Port Noarlunga Reef is home to more than 200 species of marine plants and over 50 different species of fish. Walk along the jetty until you reach the reef and enter the water in snorkel gear (or just goggles, if you're feeling brave). From the moment you enter the water, you have 5 minutes to capture, on camera, at least 5 different species of fish.
- **Smuggle Sugar to the Sugarloaf** — In the 1800s, Hallett Cove was used to smuggle goods into Australia. Travel to the beginning of the path to the Sugarloaf in Hallett Cove and hide a half cup of sugar under your hat. Walk to the end of the path without touching your hat, and without the sugar spilling. If, at any point, you touch your hat or spill the sugar, this challenge is failed.
- **Master the Dewey Decimal System** — Visit any local library. From the moment you step foot in the building, you have three minutes to find a copy of the famous Australian novel, Picnic at Hanging Rock by Joan Lindsay. You may do general research about the Dewey Decimal System or the book, but not about the library itself.
- **Predict a Point of Beach Volleyball**
- **Identify Chocolates at Haigh's Chocolate Factory** — Southern Australia is the birthplace of Australian chocolate. Visit Haigh's Chocolate Factory and, with one team member waiting outside, purchase seven filled individual chocolates. The team member waiting outside will be told the options, then must then try each chocolate and successfully guess the flavor of its filling. If any guesses are wrong, this challenge is failed.
- **Test the Honesty of the Australian People** — Buy a wallet and put $50 in it. Put it somewhere it will be taken within the next 10 minutes. If nobody takes the wallet within 10 minutes, you fail this challenge.
- **Complete 10 Obstacles on the MegaClimb**
- **Dump One Ocean in Another Ocean** — Australia lies at the intersection of three different oceans: the Pacific, the Indian, and the Southern. Collect at least six ounces of one of those three oceans and, before the day is over, dump it into a different ocean. If the day ends or you lose the ounce of ocean before you can accomplish this, then this challenge is failed.
- **Build A Car At Albert Park Circuit** — Construct a car no larger than one square foot. On a flat section of Albert Park Circuit-the site of Formula One races-the car must, with one push, successfully cover a distance of 35 feet. You get one attempt. You may not do any practice attempts longer than five feet. You may not use any materials traveling with you.
- **Keep A Ball Inbounds At 100 Steps of Federation**
- **Predict a Popular Brighton Beach Bathing Box** — At the 93 iconic Brighton Beach Bathing Boxes, identify a box that you believe will be a more popular photo location than the box next to it. See which of the two boxes next has a photo taken of it-if it's your box, you win. If it's the other box, you lose.
- **Be Familiar with Your Teammates' Game** — Pick a location between 250 and 500 feet away for one teammate to run to as fast as they can. The other teammate will write down a prediction for how much time it will take the runner to get to that destination. If they're off by more than 15%, this challenge is failed.
- **Find A Penguin At St. Kilda Pier**
- **Find An Indigenous Plane** — From the moment you begin this challenge, you have one hour to locate VH-XQA-the Qantas a220 painted in Indigenous style. You may not begin this challenge after noticing a plane, and you may not do research.
- **Predict The Train Doors** — At Flinders St. Station Platform 10, stand where you believe the doors of a local train will be when the train stops. If any part of you lines up with any part of the door, you win. You may observe trains before attempting. You must be in position 20 seconds before the train stops.
- **Catch a Fish** — Catch a live fish in the wild within 45 mins from when you begin fishing. It can be any size, and caught with any means. If you are in an area that requires a fishing license, you must obtain one.
- **Become a Socceroo** — Kick a ball of any size into a sock of any size from a distance of 20 feet. You get 10 attempts. You may stretch the sock in any way you wish but it must be stationary. Anything labeled a "sock" or "stocking" is acceptable.
- **Memorize The Flags At Commonwealth Place** — The International Flag Display at Commonwealth Place shows off 110 flags of countries that have a diplomatic presence in Australia. 10 sequential flags from this display will be chosen at random. You must correctly identify eight without consulting anything. You may study as long as you wish.
- **Win Eight Coin Tosses At The Royal Australian Mint** — At the Royal Australian Mint, flip an Australian coin eight times, guessing which side it will land on while it's in the air every time. You must get it right eight times in a row to win the challenge. You have 30 minutes.
- **Finish a Puzzle** — Acquire and complete a jigsaw puzzle of at least 250 pieces. If you don't finish the puzzle in 60 minutes, this challenge is failed.
- **Get A Hole In One At The Yarralumla Play Station** — Get a hole in one at one of Australia's largest mini golf courses. Each teammate may try each hole once. It may not be a gimmick hole that creates automatic holes in one.
- **Trap a Bug in Vegemite** — Vegemite is often used to make homemade fly traps. Make a fly trap using Vegemite and deploy it wherever you like. Once deployed, it must catch a bug within 10 minutes.
- **Make A Recognizable Portrait At The National Portrait Gallery** — Create your own version of a portrait in the National Portrait Gallery. The portrait may not have words in it. Create a google form with the photo, and the question "Who is this a drawing of?" The correct answer must be in the top two. You may disregard obvious joke answers.
- **Pass Amy's Questacon Quiz** — Go to Questacon, Canberra's national children's science museum, and spend as much time there as you like. When you leave, you must pass the science test Amy wrote, all of the answers for which were findable in the museum.
- **Spot Animals at Alice Springs Desert Park** — Alice Springs Desert Park is full of free-roaming wild desert animals. You have 1 hour to score 50 animal points. Animal points are awarded for photographing free-roaming (not captive) animals. Birds are worth 1 point. Reptiles are worth 5 points. Amphibians are worth 5 points. Mammals are worth 10 points.
- **Send a Telegraph at the Northern Territory's Oldest Telegraph** — Build a device that can, using any means, transmit Morse Code over a distance of 10 feet. Take this telegraph to the site of the Alice Springs Telegraph Station. One team member must randomly generate a 5-letter word and transmit it to the other team member in Morse Code. They must successfully decode the word on their first try.
- **Score a Bogey on Any Hole at Alice Springs Golf Club** — A bogey is one above par
- **Sense the Passage of Time** — Start a timer. Both teammates may not look at any clocks or timers until you stop this timer. You may practice, but you may not consult any regularly scheduled thing (a bus, for example) to use as a makeshift clock. You succeed if you stop this timer within 3 minutes of the 30 minute mark.
- **Teach From Afar at the World's Largest Classroom** — Alice Springs School of the Air was created to educate the sparsely-populated territory using radio technology. One team member must visit the teaching center and attend part of a lesson. After learning at least one fact, they must write down this fact and, attaching it to some kind of delivery mechanism, deliver it to their teammate while standing at least 40 feet away. The recipient may not move their feet in recovering the lesson after it has been sent, but if they cannot reach it, then this challenge is failed.
- **"Sail" Across Todd River** — Todd River is known for its distinct lack of water, and is the location of the only dry regatta in the world. Construct a "boat" that can carry one player from one side of the river to the other. If, at any point, the player touches the riverbed, this contract is void. Your teammate may not assist you. You must select a section of river that is at least 30 feet across.
- **Master the Dewey Decimal System** — Visit any local library. From the moment you step foot inside, you have three minutes to find a copy of the famous Australian novel, Picnic at Hanging Rock by Joan Lindsay. You may do general research about the Dewey Decimal System or the book, but not about the library itself.
- **Get Upcharged** — Go into any store and buy any item. Then, before entering, choose a second store. You must find the exact same item being sold for a higher price in that store. If the store doesn't have the same item, or it is being sold for less, then this challenge is failed.
- **Befriend a Quokka in under 15 Minutes** — Take a ferry to Rottnest Island. Within 15 minutes after arriving, take a picture with a quokka.
- **Carry an Egg Across the Lotterywest Federation Walkway** — Do egg on a spoon without dropping it.
- **Bet on 5 Surfers** — Go to the beach and select a surfer to bet on. On their next wave, they must stay standing for at least 3 seconds. If they fall before then, they fail. Do this 5 times. If fewer than 3 surfers succeed, the contract is voided. You must choose a different surfer each time.
