# Presidio

The Presidio is the only neighborhood on the board that's an entire national park: a
218-year-old Army post turned playground, with a Lucasfilm campus, giant Andy Goldsworthy
sculptures growing out of the forest, a working Civil War fort under the Golden Gate
Bridge, and a soldier-built pet cemetery under a freeway viaduct. Note: Crissy Field is
inside this neighborhood — the Marina Analysis Neighborhood ends at Lyon Street, and
everything west of it (Letterman, Tunnel Tops, Crissy Field, Fort Point) is Presidio
turf. The park is huge, so all six candidates cluster along the walkable
Letterman–Main Post–Crissy corridor (plus one at Fort Point on the west end).

## Candidates

### 1. Yoda Paparazzi Count
- **Do:** Find the Yoda Fountain outside Lucasfilm's headquarters. Before you're close
  enough to see who's around it, the team agrees on ONE guess: how many people (not
  counting your team) will take a photo of Yoda in the next TEN minutes? Write it down,
  then sit on the low wall and count — a phone or camera pointed at the statue counts,
  each person once. Within 2 of the true count = complete. Off? Call the next ten.
- **Where:** Yoda Fountain, One Letterman Drive (Letterman Digital Arts Center, just
  inside the Lombard Gate at Lombard & Lyon). The fountain is outdoors and accessible
  anytime — don't count on the lobby, which is weekdays-only.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (call the next ten minutes)
- **Type:** Call your shot
- **Photo:** The tally sheet held up beside the bronze Yoda, a stranger's camera raised
  behind.
- **Why here:** Lucasfilm's world headquarters is in the Presidio, and Yoda has presided
  over its fountain since 2005 — the most photographed small statue in San Francisco,
  and nobody knows the rate.

### 2. Walk the (Wood) Line
- **Do:** Find Andy Goldsworthy's *Wood Line*, a 1,200-foot serpentine line of
  eucalyptus logs snaking through the cypress grove. Each teammate must walk TWENTY
  consecutive logs without stepping off (arms out for balance — no holding hands).
  Step off = start your twenty over. Complete when every teammate has done a clean
  twenty.
- **Where:** Off Lovers' Lane near Presidio Blvd & W Pacific Ave.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (start over until clean)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** The whole team spaced along the curving line, arms out like tightrope
  walkers.
- **Why here:** A land-art sculpture you're allowed to balance on — Goldsworthy's line
  was always meant to be walked.

### 3. Good Dogs, Brave Cats, and One Weird Pet
- **Do:** At the Presidio Pet Cemetery (open daily, free — stay on the wood-chip path
  and be gentle with the graves), find and photograph headstones for THREE different
  kinds of animal, and at least one must be something that is neither a dog nor a cat
  (the 424 handmade markers include birds, fish, hamsters, and reptiles). The animal
  type must be readable from the headstone itself. Complete with all three photos.
- **Where:** McDowell Ave at Crissy Field Ave, tucked under the Highway 101 viaduct —
  look for the little white picket fence.
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** Three headstone close-ups — ideally including a beloved Army goldfish or
  parakeet — with the freeway soaring overhead.
- **Why here:** Since the 1950s, Army families buried their pets here with hand-painted
  headstones; it's one of SF's strangest and sweetest hidden landmarks.

### 4. Under the Bridge (Literally)
- **Do:** Enter Fort Point (free), climb the granite spiral staircase to the barbette
  tier (the roof), and take a photo of the team pointing up at the underside of the
  Golden Gate Bridge directly overhead. While up there, also find and photograph the
  fort's tiny rooftop lighthouse. Complete with both photos.
- **Where:** Fort Point National Historic Site, end of Marine Drive, directly beneath
  the bridge's south anchorage. Open Friday–Sunday, 10 AM–5 PM year-round — a safe bet
  for a weekend game, but check it's not a holiday closure day.
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Go there
- **Photo:** The team on a Civil War fort's roof with the entire Golden Gate Bridge
  filling the sky above them — plus SF's most overshadowed lighthouse.
- **Why here:** Fort Point is the only place in the world where you can stand on an 1861
  brick fortress while the Golden Gate Bridge arches directly over your head.

### 5. Egg Toss at the Outpost
- **Do:** At the Outpost nature playground at Presidio Tunnel Tops, find the giant wooden
  bird's nest. Stand ten paces back. Each teammate tosses an "egg" — a tennis ball, a
  pinecone, a rolled sock — into the nest. Complete when every teammate has landed one
  inside (unlimited tries). Wait your turn if others are playing in it, and collect every
  egg after.
- **Where:** The Outpost at Presidio Tunnel Tops, near the Main Post/Crissy Field
  overlook. Free; open daily 9:30 AM to 5 PM (6 PM April–October).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (toss until it lands)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** The egg mid-air over the nest, Tunnel Tops parkland behind.
- **Why here:** Tunnel Tops is SF's newest park (2022), built on top of the highway
  tunnels, and its nature playground was built from the Presidio's own fallen trees.

### 6. Crissy Field Flight School
- **Do:** Crissy Field was the Army's 1920s airfield — time for your team's first
  flight. Fold one paper airplane (bring paper or scrounge a flyer), then launch it
  from the Crissy Field grass airstrip or East Beach lawn. Success = a single throw
  that flies at least 15 paces (pace it off from launch spot to landing spot).
  You get 5 throws total, any teammates, any folds; tip — throw with the wind. Mark
  complete on a qualifying flight, failed after 5 unsuccessful throws.
- **Where:** Crissy Field East Beach / airfield lawn, off Mason Street (inside the
  Presidio — Marina territory doesn't start until Lyon Street).
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (each throw is an attempt; 5 attempts max)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** The paper airplane mid-flight (or triumphantly at its landing spot) with
  the Golden Gate Bridge in the background, pilot mid-follow-through.
- **Why here:** Crissy Field hosted historic first flights as a 1920s Army airfield;
  the restored grass airstrip is still right there under the bridge.

## Recommended trio

**1, 2, 5** — Call how many strangers will photograph Yoda in ten minutes, balance
twenty logs each along Goldsworthy's Wood Line, and toss an egg into the giant nest at
Tunnel Tops. One prediction, one balance skill, one throwing game; nothing failable,
zero strangers, all $0, ≈ 35–45 minutes. (The pet cemetery hunt and paper-plane flight
school stay as alternates.)
