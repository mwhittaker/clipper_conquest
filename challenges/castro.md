# Castro/Upper Market

The Castro/Upper Market Analysis Neighborhood is Eureka Valley plus its hilly upper flanks —
Corona Heights, Kite Hill, and the stairway streets climbing toward (but not including) Twin
Peaks, whose summit sits in the separate "Twin Peaks" Analysis Neighborhood. It's anchored by
the Castro & Market hub: the giant Gilbert Baker pride flag at Harvey Milk Plaza, the 1922
Castro Theatre, rainbow crosswalks at 18th & Castro, the F-line's vintage-streetcar terminus,
and a 1936 everything-store — with the legendary Seward Street concrete slides hidden two
blocks uphill.

## Candidates

### 1. Marquee Recall at the Castro Theatre
- **Do:** Stand across Castro St from the Castro Theatre's giant neon blade sign and
  marquee. Everyone studies the marquee for exactly 30 seconds — every title, date and
  showtime on it — then the whole team turns its back. One teammate at a time names ONE
  item from the marquee; the team keeps a written list. A repeat or anything not on the
  marquee ends the round. Turn around and check: complete when the list matches every
  line on the marquee. Short? New 30-second look, list starts over.
- **Where:** Castro Theatre, 429 Castro St (view from the opposite sidewalk).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (re-study and start the list over)
- **Type:** Memory
- **Photo:** The team with their backs to the marquee, the written list held up.
- **Why here:** The 1922 movie palace is the neighborhood's crown, and its marquee
  changes with every program — nobody can study it before game day.

### 2. Call the Color of the Next Streetcar
- **Do:** Go to the F-line terminus at Jane Warner Plaza (17th/Castro/Market). Each
  vintage PCC car is painted in the livery of a different city — cream, green, orange,
  blue, red, two-tone. Before the next car's colors are visible coming up Market, the
  team calls ONE main body color out loud on video. Right = complete. Wrong = new call
  for the next car. Cars come every 10–15 minutes.
- **Where:** Jane Warner Plaza / F-line boarding island, Castro St at 17th & Market,
  under the giant pride flag.
- **Time:** 5–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (one call per car; keep calling)
- **Type:** Call your shot
- **Photo:** The team pointing at the arriving car, its livery filling the frame.
- **Why here:** The Castro terminus is where SF's rolling museum of world streetcars
  turns around — a paint-color lottery every quarter hour.

### 3. Sidewalk Hall of Fame
- **Do:** Find three different bronze Rainbow Honor Walk plaques set into the sidewalks of
  Castro St (and Market St nearby). For each, photograph it and note from the plaque text one
  thing that person is famous for. Complete with three plaques photographed and three facts
  recorded.
- **Where:** Sidewalks along Castro St between Market and 19th St, both sides; more on
  Market St near Castro.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** A shoes-around-the-plaque shot of your favorite of the three honorees.
- **Why here:** The Rainbow Honor Walk is the Castro's own walk of fame — 40-plus bronze
  portraits of LGBTQ pioneers embedded in these exact sidewalks and nowhere else on Earth.

### 4. Twenty Questions at Cliff's
- **Do:** Cliff's Variety has sold a bit of everything since 1936. One teammate, the
  shopper, goes in alone and buys ONE item for $5 or less, then hides it in a bag. The
  rest of the team gets TWENTY yes-or-no questions to name what it is. Named within
  twenty = complete. Stumped = new shopper, new item, try again.
- **Where:** Cliff's Variety, 479 Castro St (between 17th & 18th).
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** ≤ $5 per attempt
- **Failable:** retryable (one item per attempt)
- **Type:** Word game
- **Photo:** The big reveal: the item pulled from the bag, the team mid-groan or
  mid-cheer, Cliff's storefront behind.
- **Why here:** A 1936 hardware-and-everything store is the perfect place to buy the
  most unguessable $5 object in the city.

### 5. Red Rock Lookout
- **Do:** Climb to the bare red-chert summit of Corona Heights Park. Take one photo from the
  top in which BOTH Sutro Tower and the downtown skyline (Salesforce Tower counts) are
  visible in the frame with at least one teammate. Complete when the photo shows all three.
- **Where:** Corona Heights Park summit; enter from Roosevelt Way or via Museum Way off
  Roosevelt. Open dawn to dusk, every day.
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (fog could hide the skyline — if so, wait or bail; it's rarely both
  directions at once)
- **Type:** Frame the shot
- **Photo:** Teammate arms-spread on the red rocks, Sutro Tower one side, downtown the other.
- **Why here:** Corona Heights' craggy quarried summit is the Castro/Upper Market
  neighborhood's own peak — the closest thing to Twin Peaks you can claim without leaving
  the territory.

### 6. Walk the Rainbow
- **Do:** At 18th & Castro, the crosswalks are painted in rainbow stripes that run the
  length of the crossing. Each teammate picks a different color stripe. When the WALK
  signal comes on, everyone crosses together with both feet staying on their own color
  the whole way — no stepping onto a neighbor's stripe. Anyone steps off, the whole team
  turns around and recrosses on the next WALK signal. Complete when the team makes one
  clean crossing. Cross only on the signal, and keep moving with the crowd.
- **Where:** The rainbow crosswalks at 18th St & Castro St.
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (recross on the next signal until it's clean)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** Shot from the curb: the team mid-crossing, one person per stripe, the
  giant rainbow flag up the block.
- **Why here:** The Castro painted its crosswalks rainbow in 2014, and they double as
  the city's only balance-beam course with a WALK signal.

## Recommended trio

**1, 4, 6** — Recite the Castro Theatre's marquee from memory, play twenty questions
over a $5 mystery item from a 1936 variety store, then cross the rainbow crosswalk with
every teammate on their own stripe. A memory game, a guessing game, a balance game; one
retryable, zero strangers, ~$5, ≈ 30–40 minutes.
