# South of Market

SoMa is the big-shouldered grid south of Market Street — the official Analysis
Neighborhood runs from Market down to Division/13th, and does NOT include the
waterfront (South Beach, Oracle Park, and the Embarcadero fall in Financial
District/South Beach; Mission Bay is its own neighborhood). Offices are dead on
weekends, so everything below anchors on the weekend-alive core: Yerba Buena Gardens
(open daily 6am–10pm, free), the Old Mint at 5th, and the SOMA Pilipinas cultural
district's hero-named streets between 3rd and 4th.

### 1. Ride the 1906 Survivor
- **Do:** Buy carousel tickets ($3/rider) and ride a full cycle of the LeRoy King
  Carousel — the hand-carved 1906 Charles Looff carousel that once spun at Playland
  at the Beach. At least two teammates must ride (riders under 42" need a paying
  companion). Complete when the
  ride ends.
- **Where:** Children's Creativity Museum carousel pavilion, 701 Howard St at 4th
  (Yerba Buena Gardens). Weekends 10am–4pm — no museum admission needed for the
  carousel.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** ~$6 ($3 per rider)
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Go there
- **Photo:** Teammates mid-spin on their carved animals, organ pipes and painted
  menagerie in frame.
- **Why here:** A 120-year-old carousel that outlived Playland itself, parked in the
  middle of SoMa under a Douglas Hollis "Whirligig" wind sculpture.

### 2. Behind the Waterfall
- **Do:** Find the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial and walk the passage BEHIND its
  50-foot curtain of falling water. The glass panels there carry Dr. King's words in
  many languages, honoring SF's sister cities — several aren't in the Latin alphabet.
  Phones away: no translation apps, no searching, no asking. As a team, identify FIVE
  of the panels' languages by name, and commit to all five in one photographed list
  before checking anything. Then verify (panels are captioned, or check afterward).
  All five right = complete. Any wrong = failed. One shot.
- **Where:** Yerba Buena Gardens, 750 Howard St side of the Great Lawn, between 3rd
  and 4th. Gardens open 6am–10pm daily, free.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** one-shot
- **Type:** Puzzle
- **Photo:** The team's written list of five languages held up in front of the panels,
  sheet of water blurring behind.
- **Why here:** SF's biggest MLK memorial hides its message where you'd never look —
  behind a waterfall on a rooftop garden built over a convention center.

### 3. Shake Hands, Then Dance
- **Do:** Two-stop statue tour of Yerba Buena Gardens: (a) find "Shaking Man," the
  bronze businessman with a rippled, many-faced head who eternally offers a
  handshake, and have every teammate shake his hand; (b) find Keith Haring's
  "Three Dancing Figures" and have three teammates match the figures' poses exactly.
  Complete when both photos are taken.
- **Where:** Both in/at Yerba Buena Gardens — Shaking Man near the 750 Howard St
  esplanade, the Haring figures at Howard & 3rd by Moscone.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** Two shots — a mid-handshake with the bronze man, and your trio frozen
  in the Haring dancers' poses beside them.
- **Why here:** SoMa's public art is weird and touchable: a statue you're supposed to
  shake hands with, and a Keith Haring you're allowed to dance at.

### 4. Call It at the Mint
- **Do:** Stand on the 5th Street steps (or Mint Plaza side) of the Old Mint — the
  1874 "Granite Lady" that survived the 1906 earthquake and fire while holding
  roughly a third of the nation's gold reserve. Flip a coin and call it in the air;
  complete the challenge by calling it correctly 5 times IN A ROW. A miss resets the
  streak — keep flipping until you hit it. Bring your own quarter.
- **Where:** Old San Francisco Mint, 88 5th St at Mission; Mint Plaza is the quieter
  flank.
- **Time:** 5–15 min | **Cost:** $0 (plus one non-refundable quarter of suspense)
- **Failable:** no (persistence — the streak resets, the challenge doesn't)
- **Type:** Pure luck
- **Photo:** The winning flip: coin on palm, granite columns of the Mint rising
  behind.
- **Why here:** Calling coins at the very building that struck them — SoMa's homage
  to Jet Lag's Royal Australian Mint coin flip.

### 5. Hero Streets, No Maps
- **Do:** The little streets between 3rd, 4th, Folsom, and Harrison are named for
  Philippine national heroes. Stand at Folsom & 4th and read this list aloud once:
  **Lapu Lapu, Rizal, Mabini, Bonifacio, Tandang Sora.** Then all phones become
  cameras ONLY — no maps, no searching, no asking. You have 15 minutes to find and
  photograph the team under THREE of the five hero street signs by pure wandering
  and street-sense. Clock expires = 10-minute cooldown, then restart (prior photos
  void — but what you learned is yours to keep).
- **Where:** SOMA Pilipinas hero streets, inside 3rd/4th/Folsom/Harrison
- **Time:** 15–25 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (one 15-minute no-maps window per attempt)
- **Type:** Memory
- **Photo:** The team pointing up at a blade reading LAPU LAPU, timer screenshot in
  the corner of the upload.
- **Why here:** The only street grid in America that doubles as a roll call of
  Philippine heroes — and it's just small enough to find blind, if you're sharp.

### 6. Gold-Medal Form
- **Do:** Go to Victoria Manalo Draves Park, named for the SoMa-born diver who won
  BOTH springboard and platform gold at the 1948 London Olympics — the first Asian
  American woman to win Olympic gold. On the lawn or playground (feet-on-the-ground
  or a small hop only), every teammate strikes a mid-air "perfect dive" pose — arms
  in a point overhead, toes together. Complete when you've captured the whole team
  in dive form in one frame, park sign or playground visible.
- **Where:** Victoria Manalo Draves Park, Folsom St at Sherman (between 6th & 7th),
  next to Bessie Carmichael School.
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** The team lined up in synchronized-diving poses (jumping counts as
  mid-air), park sign in frame — a podium shot 78 years in the making.
- **Why here:** A SoMa-born diver out-dove the world in 1948; her park is one of
  the only true green squares in western SoMa.

## Recommended trio

**1, 4, 2** — Ride the 1906 carousel (the trio's pure-joy entry), call five coin flips
in a row at the Mint that struck them, then the one-shot language test behind the MLK
waterfall — phones away, five languages, all-or-nothing. One ride, one streak of nerve,
one gamble; exactly one one-shot, zero strangers, ~$6 total, ≈ 30–45 minutes.
