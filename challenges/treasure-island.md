# Treasure Island

A flat, man-made island built from bay sand in 1936–37 to host the 1939 Golden Gate
International Exposition — the "Magic City" — then a Navy base, now a neighborhood-in-progress
with the best free skyline panorama in San Francisco. Anchors: Streamline Moderne Building One
(the old fair Administration Building, with a free museum lobby and restored 1939 fair statues
out front), the western-shore seawall along Avenue of the Palms, Clipper Cove (where Pan Am's
China Clipper flying boats took off — a gift of a name for this game), and Yerba Buena Island's
brand-new hilltop Panorama Park with Hiroshi Sugimoto's 69-foot "Point of Infinity" spire.

## Candidates

### 1. Touch the Sky at the Point of Infinity
- **Do:** Get to Panorama Park on Yerba Buena Island, home of the 69-foot "Point of
  Infinity" spire. Find the one spot on the hilltop where, in a single photo, the
  spire's tip appears to touch exactly the top of Salesforce Tower (or the Transamerica
  Pyramid's spire) across the bay — two points meeting, no gap, no overlap. Complete
  when the photo shows the two tips kissing. (The Muni 25 stops on Yerba Buena Island
  on its loop, or it's a ~12-minute uphill walk from the Treasure Island flats — an easy
  paved climb.)
- **Where:** Panorama Park, hilltop of Yerba Buena Island (opened 2024, free, open daily)
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (keep hunting for the angle; if fog hides downtown, a Bay Bridge
  tower top counts as the backup target)
- **Type:** Frame the shot
- **Photo:** The two tips meeting — a 69-foot needle and a 1,070-foot tower, joined.
- **Why here:** Sugimoto engineered the sculpture to taper toward a mathematically
  infinite point — this is the one place in SF you can line infinity up with the
  skyline.

### 2. Statue Charades, Class of 1939
- **Do:** On the lawn in front of Building One stand restored statues from the 1939
  fair's Pacific Unity sculpture court. Walk the lawn and study their poses. Number the statues from the left. Then one teammate, the performer, generates a
  random number on a phone (re-roll repeats) and freezes in that statue's exact pose; the
  team gets ONE guess, pointing at the statue. Right = next performer. Wrong = new
  statue, streak restarts. Complete at three in a row. (If the statues are fenced,
  play from outside the fence.)
- **Where:** Building One lawn, 1 Avenue of the Palms (just past the island's Moderne
  guard-tower entrance)
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (streak restarts until three)
- **Type:** Act it, guess it
- **Photo:** The performer frozen beside the statue they became.
- **Why here:** These are literal survivors of the World's Fair this island was built
  for — and they hold still for you to copy.

### 3. Skyline Roll Call
- **Do:** From the western-shore seawall walkway along Avenue of the Palms, spot and photograph
  (zoom is fine) 4 of these 5 landmarks: the Transamerica Pyramid, Salesforce Tower, Coit Tower,
  Alcatraz, and the Golden Gate Bridge. Complete when you have all four in photos taken from the
  seawall (one wide labeled shot or four zoomed shots both count).
- **Where:** Western shore of Treasure Island, seawall along Avenue of the Palms (any point
  between Building One and the Great Lawn)
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (fog can slow you down, but the near landmarks always show)
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** The full postcard panorama with the team in front — the exact view the 1939 fair
  was built to show off
- **Why here:** This shoreline is the city's mirror: nowhere else do you face the entire SF
  skyline head-on at water level.

### 4. Paper Clipper Airfield
- **Do:** In the 1930s, Pan Am's China Clipper flying boats took off from Clipper Cove. Fold
  one paper airplane per teammate (bring paper). From a marked line at Clipper Cove's beach or
  lawn, each teammate throws; the challenge is complete when any plane flies at least 8 walking
  paces before touching down. You get 3 rounds of throws total. Retrieve every plane — leave no
  paper behind.
- **Where:** Clipper Cove Beach / marina shoreline, between Treasure Island and Yerba Buena
  Island (off Treasure Island Road at the causeway)
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (each round of team throws = one attempt; 3 rounds max)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** A plane mid-launch over the cove, Bay Bridge in the background
- **Why here:** You're re-flying the China Clipper from its actual home water — and it's the
  cove this game's name winks at.

### 5. Find the Magic City's Receipts
- **Do:** Two finds, both at Building One: (a) photograph the bronze California Historical
  Landmark No. 987 plaque commemorating the island's 1936–37 construction and the Exposition;
  (b) step into Building One's free museum lobby (open weekends noon–7) and photograph any
  exhibit image or artifact showing the fair's long-gone 400-foot Tower of the Sun. Complete
  with both photos.
- **Where:** Building One, 39 Treasure Island Road / 1 Avenue of the Palms
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** A teammate pointing from the plaque toward the empty spot on the island where the
  Tower of the Sun once stood, exhibit photo in hand
- **Why here:** The whole island is the artifact — the plaque and lobby are where its origin
  story is written down.

### 6. TreasureFest Bounty (schedule-dependent — verify before game day)
- **Do:** TreasureFest, the island's flea-market festival running since 2011, still operates
  but only on select weekends and no longer publishes a fixed monthly calendar — confirm the
  date before including this challenge. If it's on: enter and buy any single item for $5 or
  less that is either gold-colored or pirate/treasure-themed. Complete on purchase.
- **Where:** TreasureFest grounds, Avenue of the Palms
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** ~$5 (plus any gate fee — another reason to verify)
- **Failable:** no (persistence — some vendor always has a dollar trinket)
- **Type:** Eat & buy
- **Photo:** The treasure held aloft at the vendor stall
- **Why here:** A flea market literally named for hunting treasure, on Treasure Island.

## Recommended trio

**1, 3, 4** — Three different waterfronts of the two-island neighborhood: the hilltop
park on Yerba Buena (line the Point of Infinity's tip up with the top of the skyline),
the western seawall (spot four landmarks head-on), and Clipper Cove (fly paper planes
from the China Clipper's home water). Everything is outdoors, free, and 24/7-reliable;
exactly one failable (the retryable paper airplane), zero stranger-dependent; total
on-island time roughly 30–40 minutes including the YBI climb.
