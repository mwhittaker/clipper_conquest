# Mission

The Mission is SF's oldest neighborhood and its loudest canvas: the 1776 adobe Mission
Dolores (the oldest building in the city), the mural-lined alleys of Balmy and Clarion,
the 24th Street Latino Cultural District with its panaderías and taquerias, and Dolores
Park with its skyline lawn and gold-painted 1906 hero hydrant. Challenges anchor on
those five pillars — murals you hunt through, history you spell out with your fingers,
and pan dulce you actually get to eat.

## Candidates

### 1. Pose Detective
- **Do:** In Clarion Alley, one teammate — the performer — secretly picks any human
  figure painted in any mural, then walks to the alley entrance and freezes in that
  figure's pose. The rest of the team walks the alley and must point at the right
  figure. Two guesses total. Right = complete. Two misses = new performer, new figure,
  try again.
- **Where:** Clarion Alley, between Mission St and Valencia St (parallel to 17th & 18th).
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (new performer and figure per attempt)
- **Type:** Act it, guess it
- **Photo:** Performer and mural figure side by side, identical, with the team pointing.
- **Why here:** Clarion Alley has been a free, ever-changing open-air gallery since 1992
  — packed with figures practically begging for stand-ins.

### 2. The Hydrant That Saved the Mission
- **Do:** Find the golden fire hydrant at Church & 20th, the corner of Dolores Park — the
  hydrant that kept working after the 1906 earthquake and saved the Mission from burning.
  Team photo "pumping" it bucket-brigade style, then climb to the park's upper lawn for
  the skyline.
- **Where:** SW corner of Dolores Park, Church St & 20th St.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** The team working the golden hydrant, then the postcard skyline from the top
  of the park.
- **Why here:** One gold hydrant is the reason the Mission's Victorians survived 1906 —
  it gets a fresh coat of gold every April 18.

### 3. Balmy Alley Bestiary
- **Do:** Walk Balmy Alley and photograph SIX different animals painted in the murals.
  No two animals from the same mural. Complete at six.
- **Where:** Balmy Alley, between 24th St and 25th St (parallel to Treat & Harrison).
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (murals rotate, but the alley always teems with creatures)
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** Six shots, each a painted animal with a team finger pointing at it.
- **Why here:** Balmy Alley is the birthplace of the Mission mural movement — the
  densest block of murals in SF.

### 4. Concha on the Serpent's Back
- **Do:** Buy one concha at a 24th Street panadería. The carrier balances the paper bag
  on their head and walks it two blocks to the 24th & York Mini Park — no hands. Drop
  it = walk back to the bakery door and restart. Deliver it to the giant mosaic
  Quetzalcoatl serpent and share it astride the serpent.
- **Where:** La Reyna Bakery, 3114 24th St (or Panadería La Mejor, 3329 24th St) to the
  24th & York Mini Park.
- **Time:** 15 min | **Cost:** ~$3
- **Failable:** no (restart until it arrives)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** Team lined up on the tiled feathered serpent, concha held seashell-side out.
- **Why here:** Pan dulce from a decades-old panadería plus the serpent that has guarded
  24th Street since 1972.

### 5. Spell 1776
- **Do:** At Mission Dolores, the oldest building in San Francisco, find the founding
  year 1776 on an outdoor plaque, then line up in front of the white adobe facade with
  fingers spelling 1-7-7-6.
- **Where:** Mission Dolores, Dolores St at 16th St (sidewalk view).
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** The finger-spelled 1-7-7-6 lineup in front of a building older than the
  Constitution.
- **Why here:** The neighborhood is named after this building.

### 6. Pirate Supply Run
- **Do:** Enter the pirate supply store at 826 Valencia and buy any one pirate item for
  $10 or less. While inside, find the fish theater.
- **Where:** 826 Valencia St (between 19th & 20th). Open daily 12–6pm.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** ~$5–10 (funds 826 Valencia's free tutoring)
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Eat & buy
- **Photo:** Freshly outfitted pirate under the 826 Valencia sign.
- **Why here:** A functioning pirate supply shop hidden on Valencia is the Mission's
  best "wait, that exists?"

## Recommended trio

**1, 3, 4** — Guess the posed mural figure in Clarion Alley, collect six painted animals
in Balmy Alley, and balance a concha on your head two blocks to the Quetzalcoatl
serpent. Charades, a find, a balance walk; one retryable, zero strangers, ~$3,
≈ 35–45 minutes.
