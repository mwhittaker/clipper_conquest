# Noe Valley

Stroller-and-dog paradise centered on the 24th Street shopping strip, ringed by ferociously
steep hills and blocks of pastel Victorians — and home to the only town square in San
Francisco (plus its nationally famous $1.7M toilet saga). I anchored on 24th Street's
one-of-a-kind shops, the Town Square, the 31%-grade block of 22nd Street, and a meta gift:
Clipper Street — named for the Gold Rush clipper ships — runs right through the middle of
the neighborhood. (Boundary check done against the official Analysis Neighborhood polygon:
Billy Goat Hill and Upper Noe Rec Center are officially Glen Park, so nothing here uses
them; everything below is confirmed inside Noe Valley.)

## Candidates

### 1. Spell CLIPPER
- **Do:** Noe Valley is home to Clipper Street — the game's namesake. Walk 24th Street
  and photograph the letters C, L, I, P, P, E, R, IN ORDER, each from a DIFFERENT sign,
  storefront, or poster (one letter per sign; the two P's must come from two different
  signs). Finish with the team under the Clipper Street sign at Clipper & Noe.
- **Where:** 24th St between Church and Diamond, then Clipper St & Noe St.
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Word game
- **Photo:** The seven letter shots as a strip, plus the team saluting under the
  CLIPPER street sign.
- **Why here:** You're playing Clipper Conquest; Noe Valley has Clipper Street. Some
  challenges write themselves.

### 2. Unanimous on the 31% Leg-Burner
- **Do:** Walk up the single block of 22nd Street from Church Street to Vicksburg Street
  — at roughly 31.5% grade it's tied for the steepest street block in San Francisco, so
  steep the sidewalks are staircases. Every teammate counts the sidewalk steps silently
  on the way up. At the Vicksburg end, everyone says their number at the same instant.
  All match = complete. Any disagreement = count again on the way down, then say your
  numbers at the bottom.
- **Where:** 22nd St between Church St and Vicksburg St
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (recount until unanimous)
- **Type:** Mind meld
- **Photo:** Everyone holding up the matching number at the top, the staircase-sidewalk
  dropping away behind.
- **Why here:** The steepest block in Noe Valley is built as stairs, and a stair is the
  only hill you can count.

### 3. Tin Hunt at Chocolate Covered
- **Do:** Inside Chocolate Covered (a tiny shop lined floor-to-ceiling with thousands of
  handmade tins printed with San Francisco street signs, plus 1,000+ chocolate bars from
  around the world), find and photograph a tin showing any street that crosses 24th
  Street in Noe Valley (Church, Sanchez, Noe, Castro, Vicksburg, Diamond, Hoffman...).
  Extra glory — but not required — if you find a CLIPPER tin. Then buy one chocolate bar
  made in a country no teammate has ever visited. Complete when you have the tin photo and
  the bar receipt/bar in hand.
- **Where:** Chocolate Covered, 4069 24th St (between Noe & Castro). Open daily
  10:30am–6:30pm.
- **Time:** ~15 min | **Cost:** ~$6–10 (one bar)
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** A teammate holding the street-sign tin next to the far-flung chocolate bar,
  with the wall of tins behind.
- **Why here:** This one-man shop with 5,000+ street-sign tins is a genuine
  "wait, that exists?" Noe Valley institution — and eating the bar is the reward.

### 4. Flyer Count at the Town Square
- **Do:** At the Noe Valley Town Square — the only town square in San Francisco — find
  the community message board. Before anyone gets close enough to read it, the team
  agrees on ONE guess: how many separate flyers are pinned up right now? Write it down,
  then count them together. Within 3 = complete. Off? One more guess — the number of flyers
  with a phone number on them — and count those. Miss twice = failed.
- **Where:** Noe Valley Town Square, 24th St between Sanchez and Vicksburg
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (two guesses total)
- **Type:** Guess, then measure
- **Photo:** The guess held up in front of the board, count written beside it.
- **Why here:** A former parking lot turned into the neighborhood's living room — and
  its message board is the busiest wall in Noe.

### 5. Farmers Market Yardstick *(Saturday only)*
- **Do:** At the Saturday farmers market on the Town Square (8am–1pm), buy one single
  fruit or vegetable that is LONGER than the longest shoe on your team, for $5 or less
  (leeks, daikon, and monster zucchini are your friends). Complete when the produce
  out-measures the shoe in a side-by-side photo.
- **Where:** Noe Valley Farmers Market, Town Square, 3861 24th St — Saturdays 8am–1pm
  only.
- **Time:** ~10 min | **Cost:** ~$3–5
- **Failable:** no (keep hunting the stalls until something measures up)
- **Type:** Eat & buy
- **Photo:** The vegetable lying next to the shoe on a Town Square bench, a teammate pointing
  at the winner.
- **Why here:** The Saturday market is the neighborhood's weekly heartbeat — and the
  measuring ceremony is peak farmers-market silliness.

### 6. Victorian Bingo on the Side Streets
- **Do:** On Jersey and Elizabeth Streets between Church and Castro, photograph all
  three: a house painted in three or more colors, a gable with a carved sunburst or fan
  ornament, and a pair of "twin" Victorians — two adjacent houses with matching facades.
- **Where:** Jersey St and Elizabeth St between Church and Castro.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** The three-shot set; the twins make the keeper.
- **Why here:** These blocks just off 24th are some of the best-preserved Victorian
  streetscapes in the city.

## Recommended trio

**1, 2, 3** — Spell CLIPPER letter by letter from 24th Street signs, count the
staircase-sidewalk of the 31% block until every teammate agrees, and hunt a
cross-street tin at Chocolate Covered. A word hunt, an agreement count, a find; nothing
failable, zero strangers, ≤$5, ≈ 35–45 minutes.
