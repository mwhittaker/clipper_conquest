# Nob Hill

The crest of California Street is where the Big Four railroad barons built their mansions,
and everything up here still wears their names: the Stanford Court, the Mark Hopkins, the
Huntington Hotel, and Grace Cathedral standing on Crocker's old block beside Huntington
Park's Roman fountain. Two cable car lines cross at California & Powell under a tiny
signal tower, and the neighborhood runs downhill west to the donut shops of Polk Street.
Boundary check against the official Analysis Neighborhoods map: the Cable Car Museum
(1201 Mason at Washington) falls just inside **Chinatown**, so it is NOT usable here —
but the California/Powell crossing, the crest, and Polk to Bush all are.

## Candidates

### 1. The Clockless Labyrinth
- **Do:** Grace Cathedral's outdoor labyrinth is open 24/7. Pick two walkers and one
  hidden timekeeper; the walkers give up every phone and watch. Walker one walks the
  labyrinth entrance to center — no shortcuts, step off the line and that walk restarts
  — while the timekeeper secretly times it. Then walker two walks it, trying to MATCH
  walker one's time by feel alone (they may watch walk one; no counting out loud, no
  signals from anyone). Times within 20 SECONDS of each other = complete. Wider =
  10-minute cooldown, then a fresh pair of walks.
- **Where:** Grace Cathedral's outdoor plaza, 1100 California St at Taylor
- **Time:** 10–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (one pair of walks per attempt, 10-min cooldown)
- **Type:** Mind meld
- **Photo:** Both walkers in the center rosette, the two secret times revealed side by
  side, cathedral behind.
- **Why here:** An 800-year-old Chartres pattern turned into a pacing duel — two people
  trying to share one internal clock on the barons' hill.

### 2. The Big Four in Four Frames
- **Do:** The Big Four railroad barons all had mansions on this crest, and each is still
  named here. Photograph the names **STANFORD**, **HOPKINS**, and **HUNTINGTON** on three
  different signs (hotel signage, park sign — any sign or plaque counts, one name per
  photo). For the fourth baron, Crocker, take a team photo at Grace Cathedral's golden
  front doors — the cathedral stands on Charles Crocker's old mansion block. Done at
  four photos.
- **Where:** All within two blocks of California St between Powell and Taylor (Stanford
  Court at 905 California, Mark Hopkins at 999 California, Huntington Hotel / Huntington
  Park at California & Taylor, Grace Cathedral at 1100 California).
- **Time:** ~15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** Four-shot set: three baron names in signage, plus the team in front of the
  gilded Ghiberti "Gates of Paradise" replica doors.
- **Why here:** The entire Gilded Age story of Nob Hill told in one two-block walk — the
  1906 fire took the mansions, but the names never left.

### 3. Strike the Fountain Pose
- **Do:** Find the Fountain of the Tortoises in the center of Huntington Park (a bronze
  replica of Rome's famous Fontana delle Tartarughe). One teammate matches a bronze
  youth's exact pose — one foot up, opposite arm raised overhead — positioned so statue
  and human are side by side in the same frame. Done when the ref could pair your pose
  to the figure.
- **Where:** Huntington Park, California & Taylor (directly across from Grace Cathedral).
- **Time:** ~5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** Human and bronze youth striking the identical pose, cathedral in the
  background — an instant keeper.
- **Why here:** A Roman Renaissance fountain, gifted from a Crocker family estate,
  splashing in the middle of the barons' old front yards.

### 4. Call the Crossing
- **Do:** Go to California & Powell — where the California line's tracks cross the
  Powell lines' tracks beneath a little signal tower. Before any cable car is in sight,
  record a short video declaring your call: "POWELL" or "CALIFORNIA." The next cable
  car to enter the intersection decides it — match and you win. Miss? Make a new call
  (again before the next car appears) and keep playing until the crossing proves you
  right.
- **Where:** Corner of California St & Powell St (stay on the sidewalk by the signal
  tower).
- **Time:** 5–20 min (cars run every few minutes on weekends) | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (one call per approaching car — keep calling until you hit)
- **Type:** Call your shot
- **Photo:** The winning cable car rolling through the crossing, signal tower in view,
  the team mid-cheer.
- **Why here:** One of the only places on Earth where two cable car lines still cross —
  rides cost real money, but the drama at the crossing is free.

### 5. The Giant Donut of Polk Street
- **Do:** Go to Bob's Donuts (open 24/7 since forever) and buy ONE giant donut — the
  dinner-plate-sized one from the case. Photograph a teammate holding it up like a
  porthole with their face in the hole, under the Bob's neon sign. Then the team must
  finish the entire donut before leaving the block. Done when it's gone.
- **Where:** Bob's Donuts, 1621 Polk St (between Sacramento & Clay).
- **Time:** ~10 min | **Cost:** ~$8
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Eat & buy
- **Photo:** Face framed in the giant donut hole beneath the vintage neon sign.
- **Why here:** A 24-hour SF institution on Nob Hill's Polk Street slope, famous for its
  comically oversized donuts — the cheap-treat challenge this neighborhood was built for.

### 6. Duet with Tony
- **Do:** Find the statue of Tony Bennett outside the Fairmont Hotel (unveiled on his
  90th birthday — he first sang "I Left My Heart in San Francisco" inside this hotel in
  1961). One teammate mirrors his pose beside the statue while the rest of the team,
  in frame, sings the line "I left my heart in San Francisco" out loud. Done when the
  posed photo is taken mid-song.
- **Where:** Fairmont Hotel entrance plaza, 950 Mason St at California.
- **Time:** ~5 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** Pose-match with bronze Tony, the Fairmont's grand entrance behind.
- **Why here:** The song that IS San Francisco premiered in this exact building; the
  statue marks the spot.

## Recommended trio

**1, 4, 5** — A pacing duel on the labyrinth at the crest, calling cable cars at the
only place on Earth two lines still cross (keep calling until the crossing agrees),
then down to Polk for the giant donut — the trio's one pure treat, earned by then.
Nothing here can be permanently failed; zero strangers, ~$8 total, ≈ 30–45 minutes.
