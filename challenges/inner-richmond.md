# Inner Richmond

The Inner Richmond is Clement Street: San Francisco's "second Chinatown," a fog-belt
main street of dumpling counters, a legendary used bookstore, a toy-store ice cream
parlor, and — hiding in plain sight at Funston — a Greek temple that houses the entire
internet's memory. Everything below sits on or within a block of Clement between
Arguello and Park Presidio, safely inside the official analysis-neighborhood
boundaries (Golden Gate Park and Lincoln Park are separate territories — nothing here
touches them).

## Candidates

### 1. Blind Architect at the Internet Archive
- **Do:** Go to the Internet Archive headquarters — a white Classical Revival temple (a
  former Christian Science church) with giant Greek columns. One teammate, the
  architect, faces the building; another, the draftsman, sits with their back to it and
  a pen. The architect describes the facade in words only — no pointing, no photos — and
  the draftsman draws it. Complete when the drawing shows the right number of columns,
  the triangular pediment, and the steps. Wrong column count = swap roles, new drawing.
- **Where:** 300 Funston Ave at Clement St (Funston = 13th Ave)
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (redraw until it matches)
- **Type:** Secret message
- **Photo:** The drawing held up beside the real facade.
- **Why here:** The Wayback Machine literally lives in this neighborhood, in a building
  chosen because it looks like the Archive's logo — now draw the logo blind.

### 2. The Chopstick Gauntlet
- **Do:** Join the line at Good Luck Dim Sum, fill out one of the famous pink order
  sheets, and buy at least 3 DIFFERENT dim sum items for under $10 total — cash only, so
  have bills ready. Then the gauntlet: on the sidewalk, every teammate must pick up one
  piece from the box with chopsticks and get it to their mouth — no fingers, no
  stabbing, no two-handed chopsticks. A drop means that teammate takes another piece
  and tries again. Complete when every teammate has landed one clean bite. (Chopsticks
  come in the bag; ask for extras.)
- **Where:** Good Luck Dim Sum, 736 Clement St (between 8th & 9th Ave); open Sat–Sun
  8:00am–4:30pm
- **Time:** 15 min (the line is part of the legend) | **Cost:** ~$8 cash
- **Failable:** no (retry until it lands — order enough dumplings)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** A teammate mid-lift, dumpling wobbling in the chopsticks, pink box open,
  Clement Street behind.
- **Why here:** A James Beard–recognized, cash-only Clement Street institution where
  a few dollars still buys a full dim sum spread — the beating heart of the "second
  Chinatown," and the fairest chopstick exam in the city.

### 3. Spell Your Colors
- **Do:** Inside Green Apple Books (est. 1967, creaky floors included), pull used
  books whose titles' FIRST letters spell your team's color — R-E-D or B-L-U-E — one
  book per letter, in order. Stack or fan the spines so the letters read top to
  bottom. Reshelve everything afterward. Complete when the photo clearly shows the
  spines spelling your color.
- **Where:** Green Apple Books, 506 Clement St at 6th Ave; open daily, weekend
  daytime included
- **Time:** 10–15 min (Blue teams earn their extra letter) | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Word game
- **Photo:** The stacked spines spelling RED or BLUE, taken in the maze of used-book
  shelves.
- **Why here:** Green Apple is the Inner Richmond's black hole of 70,000 books —
  people go in and don't come out; your team has to go in with a mission.

### 4. Pony Express
- **Do:** At Toy Boat by Jane — the toy-lined dessert cafe that's been a Clement
  Street landmark since 1983 — buy one scoop of ice cream, then put a teammate on the
  coin-operated pony in the middle of the shop and ride it. Complete when the ride
  has run and the scoop is in hand.
- **Where:** Toy Boat by Jane, 401 Clement St at 5th Ave; Sat opens 12pm, Sun 10am
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** ~$7 (scoop + ride)
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Eat & buy
- **Photo:** Teammate mid-ride on the mechanical pony, cone in hand, vintage toys on
  the shelves behind.
- **Why here:** An ice cream parlor that's also a toy museum with an indoor pony ride
  exists exactly one place in San Francisco, and it's this corner.

### 5. Creature Checklist at the Fish Emporium
- **Do:** Browse the 6th Ave Aquarium, the cavernous fish store stacked floor to
  ceiling with tanks. Photograph all three of: (a) a koi, (b) a crab or lobster or
  shrimp, and (c) a fish longer than your hand (hand in frame next to the tank for
  scale). Look only — no tapping on glass. Complete when you have all three shots.
- **Where:** 6th Ave Aquarium, 425 Clement St (between 5th & 6th Ave); open weekend
  daytime from mid-morning
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** The hand-for-scale shot next to the biggest fish you found.
- **Why here:** A free walk-through aquarium disguised as a neighborhood fish shop —
  a classic "wait, that exists?" Clement Street oddity.

### 6. The Oldest Book on Clement Street
- **Do:** Inside Green Apple Books, each teammate has five minutes to find the
  oldest-dated book they can (check the copyright page). Bring them together: the
  team's oldest must be printed before 1950. Complete when a pre-1950 date is in hand.
  Nobody found one? Five more minutes, different aisles. Reshelve everything.
- **Where:** Green Apple Books, 506 Clement St at 6th Ave; open daily
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (keep digging)
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** The copyright page with the winning year, finger on it.
- **Why here:** Green Apple has 70,000 books and creaky floors; somewhere in there is
  something older than everyone on your team put together.

## Recommended trio

**2, 3, 5** — Run the chopstick gauntlet at Good Luck Dim Sum, spell your team color in
book spines at Green Apple Books, and find a koi, a crustacean and a fish longer than
your hand at the 6th Ave Aquarium. One skill test, one wordplay find, one creature
hunt; nothing failable, zero strangers, ~$8, ≈ 35–45 minutes.
