# West of Twin Peaks

The leafy quarter tucked between Portola Drive and Ocean Avenue, where San Francisco
pretends to be a storybook suburb: the fog-combed eucalyptus dome of Mount Davidson
(928 ft — the city's highest natural point, with a 103-foot concrete cross hidden in the
trees), the streetcar village of West Portal huddled around the 1918 Twin Peaks Tunnel
mouth, and a cluster of grand 1910s "residence parks" — St. Francis Wood, Forest Hill,
Balboa Terrace, Westwood Park, even a Sherwood Forest — each with its own ceremonial
gates, fountains, and stairways. Anchors: the summit, the tunnel, the village street,
the gates.

## Candidates

### 1. Summit the Hidden Cross
- **Do:** Enter Mount Davidson Park from Juanita Way or Dalewood Way and follow the
  forest trail to the summit — a gentle 10-minute walk under the eucalyptus. The
  103-foot concrete cross (lit by FDR himself via telegraph in 1934, and rumored to be
  3 feet taller than planned because the crew had leftover concrete) stays invisible in
  the fog-forest until you step into the clearing. Every teammate must touch the cross
  at the same time, AND you must find the Armenian Genocide memorial plaque at its base.
  Complete when both photos are taken.
- **Where:** Mount Davidson Park summit; trailheads at Juanita Way (north) or Dalewood
  Way (southwest).
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Go there
- **Photo:** Low-angle shot from the cross's base: the whole team, palms on the
  concrete, with all 103 feet towering out of frame above them — taken at the highest
  natural point in San Francisco.
- **Why here:** The literal top of the city, and a giant monument you can't see until
  you're standing under it — only on Mount Davidson.

### 2. Tunnel Roulette
- **Do:** Stand on the West Portal Ave sidewalk with a clear view of the ornate 1918
  Twin Peaks Tunnel mouth. Three streetcar lines — K, L, and M — all pop out of that one
  hole. Start a video and call ONE letter out loud BEFORE the next outbound train's
  headlights appear in the tunnel. If the train that emerges shows your letter: done. If
  not: failed, forever. (A shuttle or out-of-service train doesn't count — redo the
  call.)
- **Where:** Twin Peaks Tunnel west portal, West Portal Ave at Ulloa St.
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** one-shot
- **Type:** Call your shot
- **Photo:** Video freeze-frame or photo of the team pointing at the emerging train,
  its route letter legible — triumph or agony, both are keepers.
- **Why here:** The neighborhood is literally named after this tunnel mouth; it's a
  Muni-powered coin flip with three sides.

### 3. Exact Change at Shaw's
- **Do:** Shaw's has sold candy on this block since 1931. As a team, choose candy whose
  prices add up to EXACTLY $5.00 before any tax — no more, no less. No phone
  calculators; mental math and paper only. Ring it up: exactly $5.00 = complete. Off?
  Back to the shelves, re-add on paper, and try again — three register trips maximum,
  then it's failed.
- **Where:** Shaw's, 122 West Portal Ave. (If Shaw's is closed, any West Portal sweet
  shop counts.)
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $5
- **Failable:** retryable (three register trips)
- **Type:** Word game
- **Photo:** The receipt reading $5.00 held up with the candy haul, Shaw's sign behind.
- **Why here:** A candy shop older than the Golden Gate Bridge deserves a proper
  arithmetic exam.

### 4. Coin in the Fountain of St. Francis Wood
- **Do:** Walk through the grand stone entrance gateway of St. Francis Wood (St. Francis
  Blvd at Portola Dr, 1913, by the architect of Berkeley's Campanile) and down the
  boulevard's landscaped center median to the big circular fountain. From the fountain's
  rim, each teammate tosses a coin into the raised center basin — the small upper bowl,
  not the big pool. Complete when every teammate has landed one; unlimited tries.
- **Where:** St. Francis Blvd median fountain, St. Francis Wood (public sidewalks and
  medians).
- **Time:** 10–15 min including the walk | **Cost:** a few coins
- **Failable:** no (toss until it lands)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** The coin mid-air over the basin, gateway pylons up the boulevard behind.
- **Why here:** A 1913 residence park with its own Beaux-Arts fountain — a wishing well
  on a boulevard median that most of SF has never seen.

### 5. Water Up the Grand Stairway
- **Do:** In Forest Hill, climb the grand Pacheco Street stairway starting at Magellan
  Ave — a formal balustraded staircase lined with giant concrete flower urns, built so
  this hillside residence park could have its own Spanish-palace entrance. Fill a cup
  to the brim with water at the bottom. Carry it to the top
  without spilling more than a fingertip's depth — no lids, no hands over the top.
  Spilled? Back to the bottom, refill. Complete when every teammate has made a clean
  climb.
- **Where:** Pacheco St stairway, from Magellan Ave up to the top landing, Forest Hill
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0 (bring a water bottle)
- **Failable:** no (refill and retry)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** A brimming cup held level on the top landing, urns marching down behind.
- **Why here:** The most palatial staircase in the city was built for a grand entrance;
  make yours without spilling.

### 6. Robin Hood's Other Forest
- **Do:** San Francisco has a neighborhood called Sherwood Forest, tucked onto Mount
  Davidson's flank — and yes, it has a Robinhood Drive. Find a Robinhood Dr street sign
  (off Lansdale Ave, a short detour from the Dalewood Way park entrance). Then, on the
  wooded edge of Mount Davidson Park just uphill, pick a tree trunk at least 20 paces
  away and hit it with a pinecone. Every teammate must hit the trunk; unlimited tries.
- **Where:** Robinhood Dr at Lansdale Ave, then the Dalewood Way edge of Mount Davidson
  Park
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (keep throwing)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** The team under the Robinhood Dr sign, then the pinecone mid-flight.
- **Why here:** A street named for the world's most famous archer, beside a forest —
  the only place in SF the archery contest makes sense.

## Recommended trio

**1, 2, 3** — Summit Mount Davidson to the hidden cross (the trio's "the place is the
game" entry), call K, L or M before the next train pops out of the Twin Peaks Tunnel
(the neighborhood's one-shot), and hit exactly $5.00 of candy at Shaw's. One climb, one
called shot, one math puzzle; zero strangers, ~$5, ≈ 35–45 minutes.
