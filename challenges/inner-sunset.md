# Inner Sunset

Fog-kissed village neighborhood between Golden Gate Park and the hills: the 9th & Irving
food-and-shops hub (a worker-owned bakery, a bookstore, and SF's last magic shop) below,
and the mosaic-stairway country of Golden Gate Heights above. Boundary check against the
official Analysis Neighborhoods map (DataSF, point-in-polygon on the `j2bu-swwd`
polygons): the 16th Avenue Tiled Steps, the Hidden Garden Steps, and Grandview Park ALL
fall inside the official **Inner Sunset** polygon — not Sunset/Parkside — so the famous
stairways are fair game here. Golden Gate Park itself is a separate neighborhood and is
avoided.

## Candidates

### 1. Your Name in 75,000 Pieces
- **Do:** At the 16th Avenue Tiled Steps (163 mosaic risers, "sea to stars"), find one of
  the 220+ handmade sponsor name tiles (animal/fish/shell shapes with donors' names baked
  in) whose name starts with the same letter as any teammate's first name. Then climb to
  the top and touch the giant sun panel. Complete when you have the tile photo and have
  reached the sun.
- **Where:** Moraga St between 15th & 16th Ave (stair base at 16th Ave & Moraga)
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (220+ names — persistence always wins)
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** Teammate pointing at "their" name tile, mosaic sweeping up the stairs behind
  them.
- **Why here:** The neighborhood literally tiled its own names into its most famous
  landmark — you're finding the Inner Sunset's residents in the art they built.

### 2. Spot the Teammate from Turtle Hill
- **Do:** Split up at 14th Ave & Moraga. One teammate, the runner, generates two random
  numbers on a phone — a direction (1 north, 2 south, 3 east, 4 west) and a distance (1–3
  blocks) — walks to that corner and stands still on the sidewalk: no waving, no bright
  jacket held up, no texting. The rest climb the wooden stairs to the bare-sand summit
  of Grandview Park ("Turtle Hill") and must find the runner: they text the corner they
  think the runner is on. Right corner = complete. Wrong = the runner texts "no" —
  three wrong texts and the runner re-rolls a new corner while the spotters look away. (Runner: if your corner isn't visible from the summit,
  re-roll before the others reach the top.)
- **Where:** Grandview Park, stairs from 14th Ave & Moraga to the summit
- **Time:** 15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (three claims per corner, then a fresh roll)
- **Type:** Find your teammate
- **Photo:** The zoomed find — a tiny teammate on a distant corner — and the summit
  crew pointing.
- **Why here:** Turtle Hill is one of the last remnants of the ancient dunes the whole
  Sunset is built on, with a 360° view down every street — a lookout begging for a
  hide-and-spot, with the hiding place chosen by dice.

### 3. Orange Tiles Only
- **Do:** At the Hidden Garden Steps (148 mosaic risers), find the giant orange
  salamander that snakes up 26 of the steps. Every teammate climbs the salamander's
  stretch stepping ONLY where the orange of its body meets the step — never on the
  blue-and-green background tiles. Foot on the background = back to the salamander's
  tail. Complete when everyone has done a clean climb.
- **Where:** Hidden Garden Steps, 16th Ave between Kirkham and Lawton
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (back to the tail and retry)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** A foot mid-step on the orange, the salamander curling up the stairs.
- **Why here:** The second mosaic staircase of the Inner Sunset is the quieter one, and
  its salamander was built to be walked.

### 4. First-Take Magic
- **Do:** Enter Misdirections Magic Shop — San Francisco's last brick-and-mortar magic
  shop — and have one teammate, the magician, buy any beginner trick for $10 or less
  (teammates wait outside). The magician practices out of sight as long as they like,
  then announces "official take" and performs it once for the team on video. Complete if
  the trick WORKS on that take — the coin vanishes, the card is found, the rope is
  restored — with no visible flub. Flubbed? More practice, and another official take.
- **Where:** Misdirections Magic Shop, 1236 9th Ave (between Lincoln & Irving); open
  weekend daytime.
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** ≤ $10
- **Failable:** no (practice and re-take)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** The reveal, mid-gasp, with the shop's storefront behind.
- **Why here:** The city's last magic shop — the only place in the game where you buy
  a skill and have to prove it on camera.

### 5. Pizza of the Day Gamble
- **Do:** Arizmendi Bakery, a worker-owned co-op, bakes ONE vegetarian pizza a day and
  changes it daily. BEFORE anyone looks at the board or the slices, the team writes down
  TWO toppings they bet are on today's pizza. Then buy one slice and check. At least one
  of your two toppings on it = complete. Neither = failed — but you still eat the slice.
- **Where:** Arizmendi Bakery, 1331 9th Ave (between Irving & Judah).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** ~$4
- **Failable:** one-shot
- **Type:** Call your shot
- **Photo:** The written prediction next to the actual slice, verdict on everyone's face.
- **Why here:** A pizza that's different every single day is a pizza begging to be bet
  on.

### 6. Farm Hidden in the Fog Belt
- **Do:** At the Garden for the Environment — a free half-acre teaching farm-garden open
  every day dawn to dusk — find plant identification labels for three different edible
  plants: one you'd put on a pizza, one you'd put in a salad, and one fruit. Name all
  three in your claim. Complete on the third find.
- **Where:** Garden for the Environment, 1590 7th Ave (at Lawton St)
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** Team gathered around the best-labeled find, three fingers up, garden beds
  behind.
- **Why here:** A working demonstration garden has been tucked into these foggy avenues
  since 1990 — the only garden in SF dedicated to teaching regenerative growing.

## Recommended trio

**2, 3, 4** — Spot a teammate standing on a dice-chosen corner from the summit of
Turtle Hill, climb the Hidden Garden Steps on the salamander's orange tiles only, and
buy a magic trick and land it on the first official take. A hide-and-spot, a balance
game, a skill test; nothing failable, zero strangers, ≤$10, ≈ 40–50 minutes.
(Arizmendi's pizza-topping bet stays the alternate one-shot.)
