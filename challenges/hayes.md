# Hayes Valley

Hayes Valley is the neighborhood that killed a freeway and got cool: when the
earthquake-damaged Central Freeway came down, Octavia Boulevard and Patricia's Green (with
its rotating, Burning-Man-adjacent public art) grew in its place, ringed by the
boutique-and-treats strip of Hayes Street. Crucially for this game, the official SF
Analysis Neighborhood boundary was verified against DataSF (tracts 016200–016400,
016801/2): **Alamo Square Park and the Painted Ladies are inside Hayes Valley**, not
Western Addition — so Postcard Row is ours to conquer. Anchors: the Painted Ladies, the
rotating art at Patricia's Green, the alley garage where Blue Bottle began, and a
Romanesque church that is now a roller disco.

## Candidates

### 1. Painted Lady Charades
- **Do:** At Alamo Square, face Postcard Row — the seven Painted Ladies of 710–720
  Steiner. One teammate, the performer, secretly picks one of the seven at random
  (a random number 1–7 on a phone, counting from the left). Without words, letters or
  pointing, the performer acts out THAT house — its color, its trim, its windows, its
  shape, whatever they can mime. When a teammate thinks they've got it, they cross
  Steiner and touch that house's front gate or railing (sidewalk only — these are
  private homes, stay off the steps). Right house = complete. Wrong house = the
  performer draws a new number and starts over.
- **Where:** Alamo Square Park, east slope along Steiner St (between Hayes & Fulton).
  Open daily, dawn to midnight.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (new house, new mime, until someone touches the right one)
- **Type:** Act it, guess it
- **Photo:** The guesser's hand on the right gate, the performer mid-mime on the grass
  across the street, Postcard Row behind.
- **Why here:** Postcard Row officially sits in Hayes Valley (verified on the city map),
  and everyone thinks the seven houses look alike — until they have to mime one.

### 2. A Quarter Pound, By Feel
- **Do:** At Miette, the pastel patisserie on Octavia whose back wall is jars of bulk
  candy sold by weight, the team fills ONE bag from the jars trying to land on exactly a
  quarter pound (4 oz) — no scale, no phone, just hands and arguments. Lock the bag,
  then let the counter weigh it. Within a quarter ounce (3.75–4.25 oz) = complete. Off?
  Buy it anyway (that's the evidence, and it's candy), and fill a new bag, wiser. Budget
  cap $10 across all attempts.
- **Where:** Miette Patisserie & Confiserie, 449 Octavia St at Linden (open daily,
  weekend daytime included).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** ~$3–4 per attempt
- **Failable:** retryable (one weighed bag per attempt, until the $10 cap)
- **Type:** Guess, then measure
- **Photo:** The scale readout beside the bag, team faces either way.
- **Why here:** Hayes Valley's sweetest storefront — a candy wall by the ounce, a block
  from the old freeway's footprint — is the only shop in the neighborhood with a scale
  that decides the game.

### 3. The $700 Million Garage
- **Do:** Find the garage on Linden Street (315 Linden, a one-block alley off the back of
  Patricia's Green) where Blue Bottle Coffee began in 2005 as a wooden kiosk in a
  friend's garage — look for the little blue bottle. Photograph the team pointing at it.
- **Where:** Linden St between Octavia & Gough, one block south of Hayes.
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** Team pointing at an unassuming garage in an alley — the birthplace of a
  coffee empire.
- **Why here:** Third-wave coffee's origin story happened in this alley, and it still
  just looks like a garage.

### 4. Be the Art
- **Do:** Find the current rotating installation at the center of Patricia's Green
  (the Arts Commission swaps it roughly yearly). One teammate, the director, studies it;
  everyone else keeps their backs to it. Using words only — no gestures — the director
  arranges the team into a human copy of the sculpture. When the director calls "done,"
  take the photo with sculpture and copy side by side. One rebuild allowed.
- **Where:** Patricia's Green, Octavia Blvd between Hayes & Fell.
- **Time:** 5–10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Secret message
- **Photo:** The sculpture beside its blind-built human twin.
- **Why here:** This half-block park replaced a double-deck freeway, and its
  ever-changing centerpiece is the neighborhood's signature.

### 5. Holy Rollers
- **Do:** Find the 1890s church at Fillmore & Fell that is now a roller disco — the
  Church of 8 Wheels. Every teammate holds a one-foot "glide" pose (arms out, one leg
  back) at the same time for a count of five out front.
- **Where:** 554 Fillmore St at Fell St (exterior).
- **Time:** ~5 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Performance
- **Photo:** A team of imaginary skaters gliding in front of a church turned roller rink.
- **Why here:** Only Hayes Valley has a landmark church converted into a disco rink.

### 6. Hayes Street Window Bingo
- **Do:** Walking Hayes St between Franklin and Laguna, photograph shop windows (from
  the sidewalk) showing all five of: shoes, a plant, something gold, a hat, and a dog
  (real or pictured). One window per category.
- **Where:** Hayes St corridor, Franklin St to Laguna St.
- **Time:** ~15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** A five-shot set; the dog is usually the keeper.
- **Why here:** Hayes Street's boutique windows are the neighborhood's whole personality.

## Recommended trio

**1, 2, 4** — Charades with the seven Painted Ladies (touch the right gate to win), a
quarter-pound-by-feel wager at Miette's candy wall, and a human copy of Patricia's
Green's sculpture built by a director who's the only one allowed to look. Charades,
weight estimation, teamwork; one retryable, nothing one-shot, zero strangers, ~$3–4,
≈ 30–40 minutes.
