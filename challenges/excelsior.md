# Excelsior

The Excelsior is Mission Street's sunny southern stretch and maybe SF's most international
neighborhood — fittingly, its grid is literally a map of the world: streets named for
capitals (London, Paris, Naples, Moscow...) crossing avenues named for countries (Brazil,
Persia, Russia, France, Italy). "Excelsior" is Latin for "ever upward," and the
neighborhood delivers: two community-built mosaic stairways, a 1935 landmark bakery, and
two sidewalk plaques for its most famous son, Jerry Garcia. Everything below sits on or
just off the Mission corridor between Silver and Geneva — the undisputed core of the
official analysis neighborhood (McLaren Park next door is its own territory; stay out of
the park itself).

## Candidates

### 1. Paris, France (No Passport Required)
- **Do:** The Excelsior's streets are named for world capitals and their avenues for
  countries. Phones away — no maps. Find a corner where a capital meets its OWN country
  (Paris St & France Ave, Naples St & Italy Ave, or Moscow St & Russia Ave) and photograph
  the team under the sign pair. Then find two "scrambled geography" corners (e.g., Paris &
  Russia, London & Persia) and photograph those sign pairs too.
- **Where:** The Excelsior's capitals-and-countries grid, east of Mission St.
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** The team under PARIS / FRANCE, plus two scrambled-geography sign pairs.
- **Why here:** The only place in SF where you can walk from London to Persia in one
  block.

### 2. Mosaic Match in Kenny Alley
- **Do:** Find Kenny Alley — a hidden pedestrian stair alley named after a cat who lived
  there — whose 47 steps and walls are tiled as a waterfall pouring down the hillside.
  One teammate, the hider, generates a random number from 1 to 47 on a phone, walks
  alone to that step, and takes a close-up photo of the hand-sized patch of mosaic
  directly under their right foot (no landmarks in the frame — just tiles). Then the rest of the team gets the phone and has to find the exact spot. The team
  gets ONE claim per photo — a finger on the tiles, and the hider says yes or no. Wrong
  = the hider rolls a new step and shoots a new patch. Every teammate takes a turn as the
  hider. Complete when every patch has been found.
- **Where:** Kenny Alley, off the east side of Mission St between France Ave and Italy
  Ave (next to the car lot; ~4800 block of Mission).
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (a wrong claim costs a new roll and a new photo)
- **Type:** Scavenger & find
- **Photo:** A finger on the found tiles held next to the phone showing the hider's
  photo, the waterfall pouring down the steps behind.
- **Why here:** A secret stairway named for a neighborhood cat, tiled into a waterfall
  by local students — thousands of tiles, and only one patch matches.

### 3. Last Supper Charades
- **Do:** Go to the 1935 Art Deco Royal Baking Company building, whose facade is painted
  with a multi-panel "Last Supper" mural — thirteen figures, thirteen poses. Number the figures 1–13 from the left. One teammate, the performer, generates a
  random number on a phone (re-roll repeats) and freezes in that figure's exact pose
  (hands, head, lean). The team gets ONE guess at which painted figure it is — point at them.
  Right = the next teammate performs. Wrong = the performer picks a new figure and the
  streak restarts. Complete when the team gets three right in a row. Pan dulce from the
  legacy bakery inside is optional, and recommended.
- **Where:** Royal Bakery, 4773 Mission St (between Russia and France Aves). The mural
  is outdoors; the bakery is open daily, roughly 9am–8pm.
- **Time:** 10–15 min | **Cost:** $0 (optional ~$2–3 per pastry)
- **Failable:** no (the streak restarts until you hit three)
- **Type:** Act it, guess it
- **Photo:** The performer frozen mid-pose beneath the matching painted figure, the
  team pointing.
- **Why here:** A San Francisco Legacy Business baking in double-brick Dutch ovens since
  1935, under the Excelsior's most famous painted facade — thirteen poses waiting to be
  copied.

### 4. Recite for Jerry
- **Do:** The Excelsior raised Jerry Garcia of the Grateful Dead, and the neighborhood
  gave him two sidewalk plaques a long block of Mission apart. Find either bronze plaque
  (Mission & Harrington, or Mission & Amazon). One teammate reads the plaque aloud ONCE.
  Then a different teammate, eyes closed, recites its text back word for word. Any slip
  = read it again, new reciter. Complete on a clean recital.
- **Where:** Mission St at Harrington St (~4600 block) or Mission St at Amazon Ave
  (~4900 block).
- **Time:** 10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (re-read and retry)
- **Type:** Memory
- **Photo:** The reciter eyes-closed mid-line, a shoe beside the plaque.
- **Why here:** Garcia grew up at 87 Harrington St and 121 Amazon Ave; the
  plaques are short enough to memorize and long enough to trip on.

### 5. Land It on the Rainbow
- **Do:** At the Athens Avalon Greenspace, the stairway is mosaicked in a color gradient
  — cool and dark at the bottom, warm and bright at the top. Stand at the bottom step.
  A teammate calls a color band ("green!"); the thrower tosses a coin or bottle cap
  underhand and it must come to rest ON that band. Rotate throwers. Complete when the
  team lands three called bands (any three, not in a row). Pick up every coin
  afterward.
- **Where:** Athens Avalon Greenspace, 800 Avalon Ave at Athens St (four blocks up the
  hill from Mission via Avalon).
- **Time:** 10–15 min including the uphill walk | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (keep tossing)
- **Type:** Aim & throw
- **Photo:** The coin resting on the called band, the gradient climbing behind.
- **Why here:** Neighbors turned a trash-strewn hillside into SF's most joyful gradient
  stairs — "ever upward," one toss at a time.

### 6. Call the Bus at "Waiting for the 52"
- **Do:** Stand at the mural "Waiting for the 52 Excelsior" (2001, Marta Ayala) — a
  portrait of Excelsior folks waiting for the bus — and do what it shows: wait, but with
  a call. Before each bus's number is readable, the team calls its line out loud on
  video: 52, 14, 14R, or "other." Right = complete. Wrong = new call for the next bus.
- **Where:** The mural near Mission St & Excelsior Ave, at the nearest stop.
- **Time:** 5–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (one call per bus)
- **Type:** Call your shot
- **Photo:** The team in front of the mural with the verdict bus and its sign readable.
- **Why here:** A mural about waiting for a bus — the only honest way to complete it is
  to call the bus.

## Recommended trio

**1, 3, 5** — Navigate the capitals-and-countries grid with no maps, play charades
with the thirteen figures of the Last Supper mural, and land a coin on a called color
of the Athens Avalon rainbow stairs. A deduction hunt, a pose-guessing game, a tossing
game; nothing failable, zero strangers, $0 (plus optional pastry), ≈ 40–50 minutes.
