# McLaren Park

This Analysis Neighborhood is essentially John McLaren Park itself — at 312 acres SF's
second-largest park — plus the rec facilities tucked inside its lines (Louis Sutter
Playground/McNab Lake at the northeast corner, Gleneagles Golf Course to the south). No
shops, no bars, just hills: a Tiffany-blue water tower a 1956 newspaper called "a kind of
high-calorie Coit Tower," America's only trail built for philosophers, an amphitheater
named for hometown hero Jerry Garcia, and a log-tower playground with a 20-foot tube
slide. Everything below is inside the park, free, and open weekend daytime.

## Candidates

### 1. Muse Hunt on Philosopher's Way
- **Do:** Philosopher's Way is a 2.7-mile loop dotted with granite "musing station"
  plaques. Find TWO different musing stations. At each, one teammate reads the quote
  aloud ONCE; then a different teammate, without looking, must recite it back word for
  word. Any slip = read it again and retry. Complete when both quotes have been recited
  back cleanly.
- **Where:** Philosopher's Way, starting from the Overlook lot at Mansell St &
  Visitacion Ave.
- **Time:** 15–20 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (re-read and retry)
- **Type:** Memory
- **Photo:** The reciter at the granite plaque, eyes closed, mid-quote.
- **Why here:** A walking trail built for thinking — so think hard enough to remember.

### 2. Pace the High-Calorie Coit Tower
- **Do:** Hike up to the La Grande Tank, the 80-foot sky-blue water tower on the park's
  highest ridge. From the point where the tank first comes fully into view, the team
  agrees on ONE guess: how many of your tallest teammate's paces to reach its base? Write
  it down, then that teammate walks it at a normal stride, everyone counting out loud.
  Within 10% = complete. Off? One more guess — the tank's circumference in paces — and pace that. Miss twice
  = failed.
- **Where:** La Grande Tank hilltop, off the John F. Shelley Dr loop near the Wilde
  Overlook (walk uphill toward the blue thing).
- **Time:** ~12 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (two targets, two guesses)
- **Type:** Guess, then measure
- **Photo:** The pacer's hand on the tank, guess and count held up side by side.
- **Why here:** The 1956 Call-Bulletin called this tank "a kind of high-calorie Coit
  Tower"; painted eggshell blue to blend into the sky, it's famously hard to judge the
  distance to.

### 3. One Hearing at the Jerry Garcia Amphitheater
- **Do:** Go to the Jerry Garcia Amphitheater, the Greek-style bowl named for the
  Grateful Dead guitarist who grew up in the Excelsior next door. One teammate stands
  center stage; another sits in the LAST row with a pen. The stage teammate generates
  eight random words on a phone (search "random word generator"), then reads them aloud
  ONCE, in order, at any volume — no repeats, no spelling. The back-row teammate writes
  down what they heard. All eight words, in order = complete. Miss = swap roles, eight new
  words. (If an actual event is underway, come back later.)
- **Where:** Jerry Garcia Amphitheater, 40 John F. Shelley Dr, mid-park.
- **Time:** ~10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (new words until a clean eight)
- **Type:** Secret message
- **Photo:** Shot from the back row: the tiny speaker center stage, then the two
  matching lists held up together.
- **Why here:** SF's only outdoor amphitheater, named for Jerry Garcia because 87
  Harrington St is a few blocks away — the bowl is usually empty and all yours, so it's
  just your voice, the distance, and one chance to hear.

### 4. Slide Squad at Redwood Grove
- **Do:** At the Redwood Grove Playground, every single teammate climbs the multi-tiered log-and-rope tower and rides the 20-foot stainless tube slide
  from the top. Complete when the last teammate pops out the bottom.
- **Where:** Redwood Grove Playground, off John F. Shelley Dr at the group picnic area on
  the park's west side.
- **Time:** ~10 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (light climbing; take turns as needed)
- **Type:** Body
- **Photo:** The final rider emerging feet-first from the tube with the rest of the team
  arranged around the exit, cheering.
- **Why here:** McLaren's showpiece natural playground — a log tower built into the
  hillside redwood grove, with the best slide in the southeast of the city.

### 5. Walk the Labyrinth Without a Wobble
- **Do:** On Philosopher's Hill (along Philosopher's Way, near the reservoir), find the
  stone labyrinth laid into the hilltop. Each teammate must walk the winding rock-lined
  path from the entrance all the way to the center without stepping outside the path.
  Step out = that teammate returns to the entrance and starts over. Complete when every
  teammate has reached the center on a clean walk.
- **Where:** Hilltop stone labyrinth off Philosopher's Way near the Upper Reservoir
  (Philosopher's Hill).
- **Time:** ~10–15 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** retryable (an attempt = one walk from the entrance; unlimited retries)
- **Type:** Balance & carry
- **Photo:** The whole team clustered at the labyrinth's center, spiral of stones
  visible around your feet, bay view behind.
- **Why here:** A hand-laid rock labyrinth on a hilltop is one of McLaren's best-kept
  secrets — most San Franciscans don't know it exists.

### 6. Animal Charades at McNab Lake
- **Do:** At McNab Lake, find the tiled mural of the San Francisco garter snake running
  along the retaining wall. Then play charades with what's around you: one teammate
  secretly picks any animal they can see right now — on the lake, in the mural, on a
  leash — and acts it out, no sounds, no pointing. The team gets ONE guess. Right = next
  actor, new animal (no repeats). Wrong = the streak restarts. Complete at three in a
  row.
- **Where:** McNab Lake, next to Louis Sutter Playground, near University St & Wayland
  St, northeast corner of the park.
- **Time:** ~12 min | **Cost:** $0
- **Failable:** no (streak restarts until three)
- **Type:** Act it, guess it
- **Photo:** The actor mid-duck, the snake mural and the real ducks behind.
- **Why here:** A community-made mosaic honoring the SF garter snake beside the duck
  lake locals call the park's living room — a zoo's worth of charades material.

## Recommended trio

**1, 3, 4** — Recite musing-station quotes from memory on Philosopher's Way, carry a
sentence from the stage to the back row of the Jerry Garcia Amphitheater, and take the
20-foot tube slide at Redwood Grove (the pure-fun entry). A memory game, an acoustics
game, a slide; nothing failable, zero strangers, all $0, ≈ 40–50 minutes.
